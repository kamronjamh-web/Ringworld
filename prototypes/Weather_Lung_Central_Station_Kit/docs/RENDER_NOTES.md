# Actual Blender render views

All images in `previews/` are rendered from the editable Blender scene. They are not image-generation concept art.

- `01_Wide_Actual_Blender.png`: the entire central station, low-angle overview.
- `02_Intake_Bore.png`: the open tilted intake and its structural ribs/vanes.
- `03_Architecture_Top.png`: architecture-only overhead, with preview context hidden.
- `04_Maintenance_Gallery.png`: a projecting gallery and access platform near the intake.

Water, condensation ribbons, neutral context ground and hidden cloud volumes are preview-only. They are excluded from all exported GLB architecture. The render context is intentionally simple to make the model's geometry assessable.
