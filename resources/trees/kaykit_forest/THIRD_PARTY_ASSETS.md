# KayKit Forest Nature Pack assets

This folder vendors six live-tree glTF models from the official free **KayKit Forest Nature Pack 1.0** by Kay Lousberg: https://kaylousberg.itch.io/kaykit-forest

The source package is licensed **CC0**; its included `License.txt` is retained alongside these files.

Source archive SHA-256: `2EE83E63BB7695F2D884EC27DDF6FCE020789A452E7D5C5B0BBDFC4F6EA1FC8C`.

Included tree models (each with its matching `.bin` buffer):

- `Tree_1_A_Color1.gltf`
- `Tree_1_B_Color1.gltf`
- `Tree_2_A_Color1.gltf`
- `Tree_2_C_Color1.gltf`
- `Tree_3_A_Color1.gltf`
- `Tree_4_B_Color1.gltf`

`forest_texture.png` is shared by all included glTF models and is deliberately colocated so their relative texture URI remains valid. The archive and its sample, FBX, and OBJ assets are not included.
