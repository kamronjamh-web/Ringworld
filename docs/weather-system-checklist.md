## Design target

Weather evolves around the whole ring whether the player is watching or not. Clouds, precipitation, wind, lightning, and sound describe the same storms. Quiet weather and the approach/aftermath of storms matter as much as their peak. Preserve the 75-minute day and existing eclipse configuration.

Initial presentation target: the Certified ring with the default Sunshine cloud provider. Natural weather queries also run on Normal rings. CLP, Mega-preset weather, and lifecycle-driven legacy mesh rebuilds need separate integration work.

Default natural Sunshine cloud deck: 2,000–6,000 meters above the ring datum. Storm-front and anvil altitude ranges remain independent.

At most one authoritative weather query is made per frame and at most eight per real second, without catch-up bursts. Samples expire after three active seconds. The strongest valid sample sets the bed instead of summing 16 loops.
