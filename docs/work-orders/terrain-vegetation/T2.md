### T2-1. Conditioning and routing study

ROLE: sol_lead (design)
SIZE: M
DEPENDS ON: T0

OBJECTIVE
Write design sections D1-D4 of this file: grid and sampling, barriers and outlets, depression conditioning that meets the lake-share target, and flat routing without D8 artefacts. Back each choice with prototype runs, and send the user before-and-after river maps.

CONTEXT / ROOT CAUSE
- T0 8.2 found three blockers for production: about 20 % of land is lake, D8 leaves straight lines and fans on flats, and rivers are short stubs that end in lakes (F29).
- The prototype never cuts anything (F28: lakes are depressions deeper than the limit), so it cannot show what breaching does.
- Plan 3.1 step 3 asks for "breach small depressions, fill large ones", with lake abundance as a recipe field. T0 shows the basins are deep (p90 163 m), so a breach limit of 5-40 m changes nothing. Breaching must cut much deeper, which produces water gaps and gorges, or the lake share must be set by ranking depressions.
- Point samples at 1 km alias the 256 m detail (spec 7, "Drainage prototype bias"), which adds spurious pits.

FILES/SYMBOLS
- tools/LandscapeDrainagePrototype.cs (`Options`, `SampleGrid`, `BakeFlow`, `FindDepressions`, `Statistics`, `WriteMaps`).
- tools/landscape_drainage_prototype.gd (doc comment only).
- This file, section "Design", D1-D4.

REQUIRED CHANGES
1. Extend the prototype behind new command-line options. Every default must stay as it is, so a run without options reproduces T0 8.2.
   - `--seed=<n>`: build the recipe with `EmitterSiteTestWorlds.Gen17WeatherLungRecipe(seed, climate: false)`, as tools/LandscapeWorld.cs:37 does for 1337, and record the seed in the JSON.
   - `--out-phase=<name>`: output to `.godot/audit_baseline/common/<name>/drainage/<stamp>/` instead of the hard-coded `t0` (:125).
   - `--wrap`: periodic around the ring. This needs `--strip-km` equal to the circumference; report an error otherwise.
   - `--walls`: the across edges are barriers (rim walls), not grid bounds.
   - `--subsample=1|2|4`: the cell value is the mean of n x n samples.
   - `--smooth=0|1`: a 3 x 3 binomial filter on the routing height before conditioning.
   - `--conditioning=fill|breach-fill`, with `--breach-max-depth=<m>` and `--breach-max-length=<cells>`: least-cost breaching. Cut a path from each pit's outlet sill towards the nearest lower cell, lowering cells along the path, limited by depth and length. Whatever cannot be breached is filled.
   - `--lake-share-target=<fraction>`: rank the depressions by breach cost (cut volume) and breach the cheapest until the lake share of land is at or below the target. Report the residual share.
   - `--min-lake-km2=<a>`: a smaller depression is breached or filled flat with no lake.
   - `--flats=d8|barnes`: Barnes, Lehman and Mulla (2014) flat resolution, which drains towards lower edges and away from higher ones.
   - `--tie-jitter=<seed>`: a deterministic, seeded tie-break.
2. Add these statistics:
   - breach cuts: count, depth p50/p90/max, length p50/max, and the number of cuts deeper than 40 m (each becomes a water gap or gorge at runtime);
   - a straight-run artefact metric: the share of channel length in runs of 6 or more cells along one of the 8 grid directions, for the coastal belt and overall;
   - the share of channels ending in a lake;
   - the mean river length from source to outlet.
3. Run a sweep on seed 1337:
   - the T0 strip at 1 km and 500 m;
   - the whole ring at 1 km with `--wrap --walls`;
   - seed 4242 at 1 km.
   Keep the matrix small: 3 breach depths x 2 lake-share targets x 2 flat methods x 2 filters, plus the T0 default.
4. Write D1-D4 (see "Design"), each with its decision and the numbers behind it. Include a recommended `lake_abundance` mapping for D13 and the whole-ring bake time split by stage.
5. Send the user 3-4 PNGs in chat, with one-line captions: the T0 crop window before and after, the whole-ring map at the chosen setting, and an artefact close-up.

DO-NOT-CHANGE CONSTRAINTS
- Generation 1 worlds must stay identical: `world_generation_fingerprint_test` must pass unchanged.
- No production code changes.
- A default prototype run reproduces T0 8.2. Its JSON must equal `.godot/audit_baseline/common/t0/drainage/20261109_233403/drainage_1000m.json` apart from the `timing` and `run` objects.

## Design

### D1. Grid and sampling (T2-1)
- Cell size: 1 km (T0: 500 m agrees within 6 % on length).
