# T7. Lowland meso-relief (the "landforms" step): work orders

Parent: docs/terrain-vegetation-revamp-plan.md section 11 (T7). Prerequisite reading: plan sections 2.1 (relief ladder), 2.2 (world generations), 4 (landforms), 8, 12 (T7 row), 13.1 (questions 4 and 5); `docs/landscape-audit-t0-spec.md` sections 2.3 (classes) and 8.1 (relief spectrum); `docs/lowland-landform-overhaul-plan.md` sections 3, 9 and its acceptance list (lines 450-460); `docs/terrain-vegetation-handoff-2026-11-10.md`; `docs/world-generations.md` (written by T1); the T1 and T2 phase files in this folder; AGENTS.md (testing, ffmpeg, output cleanup); the `godot-test-run`, `test-scaffold`, `shader-debug` and `perf-capture` skills.
Status: not started
Depends on: T1 (recipe v9, generation ledger, feature mask, test-world tooling) and the frozen "rivers" step (T2 and T3). T7-11's community hook also reads T6's community table if T6 has landed.
Freeze: landforms

T7 adds walk-scale relief to the lowlands, which are smooth today, in the three characters the user chose: hummocks with ponds, buttes near plateau edges, and coastal cliffs with dunes. Up close they should look natural; at map scale the builders' hand shows (bench levels). T7 also adds band-limited sampling, so coarse LODs drop detail they cannot represent. Everything is behind the T1 feature mask. Generation 1 and every frozen generation stay byte-identical.

Out of scope: tors, dry pits and desert-climate dunes (plan section 4 lists them, but the user's first characters do not include them; see "User decisions" at the end), coves or any new sea area, badlands (T9d), the GLSL river carve (T9b), and small streams (T9a).

ROLE: sol_lead (design)
SIZE: M

OBJECTIVE
Write the "Design" section of this file. It must settle every open choice in plan section 4 and in decisions D1-D11 below, so that T7-3 to T7-14 can be implemented without design work.

CONTEXT / ROOT CAUSE

FILES/SYMBOLS

- Write: this file, section "Design", only. Prototype code may live under the scratchpad or `.godot/`. Do not leave it in `scripts/`.

REQUIRED CHANGES
Write one subsection per decision. Each states the choice, the formula or constants, and the reason, and cites file:line where it touches code.
1. **D1 Gating.**
   - Name the ledger features. Recommended: `meso_relief` (baseline roughness, hummocks and ponds, buttes), `coastal_variety` (cliffs, headlands, strand dunes) and `band_limited_sampling`.
   - Say how the height model asks the ledger, following T1's API. Gating goes through `TerrainGenerationParameters`, like `HasLowlandLandforms` (F4).
   - Say how the GPU gets the bits: a feature word in the recipe buffer (F11, F12), not the `ringworld_world_features` shader global.
   - Say what review worlds list in `review_features`.
2. **D2 Recipe fields.** Give names, ranges, defaults and integer/float types, following T1's rule for adding fields to v9 (F4). Recommended set, mapped to plan section 4's menu list:
   - `meso_roughness_m` and `meso_roughness_scale_m`;
   - `hummock_height_m`, `hummock_spacing_m` and `pond_abundance`;
   - `dune_height_m` and `dune_spacing_m`;
   - `butte_frequency`;
   - `coastal_cliff_share`;

   Everything else is a code constant. State which fields are hidden (no slider). If T1 gives no rule for adding fields without moving earlier generations' canonical JSON, stop: this is an escalation.
3. **D3 Terms**, each as a pure function of ring coordinates and the recipe, seam-periodic and C0 (C1 where cheap):
   - (a) **Style partition.** Weights for baseline, hummock, butte, coastal-cliff and strand-dune styles from the existing `Ruggedness` and `PlateauWeight`, the new style noise and a land-side shore distance. The weights must sum to at most 1 per point.
   - (b) **Baseline roughness.** About 2 to 6 m at 30 to 120 m wavelength (plan section 4), sized so R3 holds. Show the slope arithmetic: a sine of amplitude A and wavelength L has peak slope 2*pi*A/L.
   - (c) **Hummocks.** Cellular knolls of 8 to 25 m at 150 to 400 m spacing with closed hollows, on the `EnhancedCellGullyField` lattice pattern (F2).
   - (d) **Buttes.** Flat-topped outliers one or two bench steps high (40-160 m), just outside plateau edges. The tops snap to `Terrace` levels so they share the ring-wide datum (plan 2.3).
   - (e) **Coastal variety.**
     - The landform gate must not be widened in a way that changes `Diagnostics.LandformGate`. The T0 classifier depends on it (F15), so add a separate coastal weight and diagnostic.
     - Cliffs and headlands only *raise* land. No term creates new sea area (no coves).
     - The beach profile is unchanged outside the cliff style.
     - Say how the land-side shore distance is estimated, and what it costs: the ocean side already uses the continental gradient at lines 235-244.
   - (f) **Strand dunes.** Asymmetric ridges of 5-30 m at 100-400 m, gentle upwind and at about 32 degrees on the slip face. Coastal strand only. The wind direction must be a deterministic function of the recipe and position, not the runtime weather.
   - (g) **River fade.** All T7 terms are multiplied by (1 - corridor weight) from T2's carve lookup, with a margin so banks stay above water and lake rims stay intact (S6). Name the T2 symbol.
   - (h) **Ordering.** Natural v7 terms, then T2's carve, then the T7 terms, then the clamp, then the site operators. Say where it sits in the v9 method T1 and T2 built.
   - (i) **Floor rule for S7.** For example, where the pre-T7 height is above 0, the result is at least min(pre-T7 height, 0.5 m).
   - (j) Seed offsets for new noises (F1) and the per-worker cache, if one is used.
4. **D4 No-meso evaluation path.** The site registry source (`EmitterSiteHeightSources.cs:34`, F6) and T2's drainage bake must evaluate heights *without* any T7 term or band-limiting. That keeps emitter sites, Wall Engines, the Weather Lung and the drainage graph identical to the rivers generation of the same seed (plan section 4: "a landforms-generation world has the same rivers as the rivers-generation world of the same seed"). Specify the API, for example a model constructor option, and every caller.
5. **D5 Pond design** (the hardest point; see escalation).
   - Ponds are stateless and still: the level is a constant per hummock cell, from the cell hash and the cell's own base height (plan section 4).
   - Containment must hold **by construction**: an enforced rim annulus, or a level derived from a conservative rim bound. Sampling must not be needed to prove it.
   - Exclusions: river corridors, lake rims, site discs, the coastal belt within the beach profile, and slopes where the base tilt across the pond exceeds the hollow depth.
   - Pond level is always above 0, so a pond is Lake kind, never Ocean.
   - Give the cost: if the level needs the base height at the cell centre, say whether it is cached per worker (C#) and recomputed per invocation (GLSL), and what that does to C1 and C4.
6. **D6 Band-limit rule (CPU and GLSL).**
   - (a) The per-octave fade weight w(L, footprint). Plan: a term whose wavelength is under about 4x the footprint fades to its mean.
   - (b) Which terms it applies to: at least secondary, micro, lowland and mountain gullies, hill detail and every T7 term. Give each term's mean (gully carves fade to their mean carve, not to 0).
   - (c) **Exactness.** When no octave of a term is faded, the existing FastNoiseLite fractal object is called unchanged, so footprint 0 and the 2 m path are bit-identical to the unfiltered model. When some octave is faded, octaves are evaluated one by one: seed+i, frequency f*lac^i, the same amplitude chain as `fnl_sample` (F11), with weights.
   - (d) The footprint each caller passes (F8-F10): proxy spacing per level, the K2 biome pass at 16 m centres (F12), global LOD, the far viewport, and the pyramid.
   - (e) **Pyramid bounds.** Bounds must still contain the full-detail surface: either sample at footprint 0, or pad by the faded amplitude bound.
   - (f) **Morph and skirt.** How the radial morph target relates to the parent level's footprint. Read `BuildRawSector`'s `parentSpacing` and `globalMorphTarget` (F8). Say whether skirts stay at 8 m.
   - (g) The **lodgap metric** for C3: on a shared edge between a level at spacing s and its parent at 2s, |H(m, s) - (H(a, 2s) + H(b, 2s)) / 2| at the fine midpoint vertex m, using the T7-8 tiles.
   - (h) How T2's own carve footprint rule composes with it.
7. **D7 GLSL plan.**
   - New noises take `HM_N_*` indices from 16.
   - New `HM_P_*` words go in a block appended **after** `RC_P_COUNT` (317), so no `RC_P_*` index moves (F11).
