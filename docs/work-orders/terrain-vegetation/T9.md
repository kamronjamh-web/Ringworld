## Phase facts

Verified on branch `gpu-offload`, 2026-11-10, unless marked. Most T9 items build on T2, T3 and T7 code that does not exist yet, so each design WO re-reads the code it depends on.

- F1. **Rivers gate G1** (plan section 11, T2): "Bake within 20 s and zero coverage waits in the travel benchmark over river country, or the GLSL carve moves from T9 into T2." River and lake sectors build on the CPU (plan 3.1, est. 10-20 % of land sectors at the 2 km level). T9b may already have been absorbed by T2; check T2's phase file first.
- F2. **GLSL height model**:
  - `materials/terrain_gpu/ring_height_model.glslinc` ports the natural path only (header lines 1-9).
  - The host's `ProxyGpuHeightKernel.IsSupported` (`scripts/Terrain/Generation/Gpu/ProxyGpuHeightKernel.cs:50-58`) decides GPU eligibility.
  - The `HM_P_*` defines are the buffer contract parsed by the host (`ParseLayout`, lines 118-124).
  - `RC_P_*` occupies 185-316 (`ring_regional_climate.glslinc:16, 36`). New blocks append after 317 (T7 D7) or after whatever T7 appended.
  - K2 builds 8/16/32 m sectors (`materials/terrain_gpu/ring_proxy_sector_k2.glsl`).
- F3. **Drainage**:
  - T0's prototype found channel density 0.034-0.081 km per km² at 25-100 km² thresholds, 98.6 % of channel cells ending at the ocean, and D8 straight-line artefacts on flats (`docs/landscape-audit-t0-spec.md` 8.2).
  - The production graph is T2's (unverified until T2 lands): resident, about 16 MB, with reach hydraulics from T3.
- F4. **Small streams** (plan section 3, ideas table): "Small streams (2-20 m) with real water: contingent (T9). Needs a tile-local network; large." Trunk rivers start at the T2 catchment threshold (est. 50-100 km², plan 3.1).
- F5. **Estuaries and chutes** (plan 3.1): estuaries are "the last reach carved below 0 and the sea floods it". Chutes drop 1 m over 8-12 m. Deltas and waterfall meshes are "polish on top of T2 and T3" (plan section 3, ideas table).
- F6. **Stamps** (plan section 4, rejected alternatives): "Heightmap stamps. Strong, but adds an asset pipeline and repetition visible from altitude. Kept as a T9 option." Badlands go beyond today's "breaks" (`TerrainGenerationHeightModel.cs:275-280`, `BreaksStrength` and `BreaksStepM`).
- F7. **No caves in gen-17**:
  - `scripts/Underground/Terrain3DUndergroundProbeSource.cs:5-7`: "Caves are not ported yet, so density is 'air above the surface height, solid below'".
  - Terrain3D holes already exist for emitter apertures (`scripts/Terrain/Surface/RingTerrain3DApertureMask.cs`; collision hole mask `RingTerrain3DCollisionGeometry.cs:22, 60`). That is the precedent for a surface opening.
  - The gen-16 voxel overhang settings (`scripts/Voxel/Phase3/VoxelTerrainSettings.cs:49-51`) are legacy and are not used by gen-17.
- F8. **Placement** (plan section 8): "Placement stays on the CPU, culling on the GPU. This keeps determinism, the disk cache and collision records. GPU placement is a T9 option for ground cover only." Grass placement is `RingGrassTileBuilder.Build` (`scripts/Voxel/LOD/RingGrassTileBuilder.cs:109`), in CPU tiles of 16-48 m (plan 1.2).
- F9. **Headworks** (plan 3.1): "A great river's source is an outfall, not a spring; T2 needs no art for it, and T9 lists headworks and wall-foot spill fans as options."
  - Wall Engines come from `scripts/Terrain/Sites/ClimateMachineRegistry.cs` and are opt-in through the recipe field `climate_machines` (`TerrainGenerationParameters.cs:52`; default 0, line 99). So spill fans exist only in worlds that turn machines on.
  - POIs go through the `poi-asset-import` skill (`assets/poi/`, the shared `ring_poi_architecture` shader, `RingPoiHaze`, streaming registration).
- F10. **Valley mist**:
  - Plan 10.8: "The drainage graph holds exactly that [a basin floor field]. River-valley mist becomes a small follow-up once T2 and V4 exist."
  - `docs/vastness-plan.md` (lines 169-173):
    - V7a is the mist lid, a logistic mist profile in `ring_haze_shared.gdshaderinc`.
    - Pooling in high basins is deferred: "a low-pass 'basin floor' field from the min-max pyramid sampled in the V4 march".
    - Any edit to `ring_haze_shared.gdshaderinc` forces the Sunshine reimport routine (vastness plan line 403).
  - Light shafts (V4) are deferred (user memory, haze revamp status).
- F11. **Budget**: plan section 12 has no T9 rows, so each design WO proposes its own gates. Set them against the 1.5 ms total for all phases, the 48 draws and the 0.2 ms main-thread budget (plan section 0), and the A1000 headroom risk (plan section 14).

## Order and parallelism

Groups are independent of one another, except that T9c and T9g may share river-source and estuary data from T2. Inside a group the order is: design WO, user go on the design, the implementer WOs as the design orders them, and the freeze if visible. GPU WOs of different groups never run at the same time.

## The freeze pattern for a visible group

Each visible group ends with a freeze WO (`sol_implementer`, S) that does exactly what T7-14 does:
- the ledger row (generation N = N-1 plus the group's features) and the launcher default;
- the fingerprint row appended with `--capture`;
- a recipe-freeze row if heights changed;
- two or three look goldens per machine;
- `world_generation_contract_test`;
- the test world `g<NN>-<step>-s1337` via `tools/new_generation_world.ps1`;
- review worlds deleted;
- `docs/world-generations.md` updated.

Its validation is `world_generation_fingerprint_test`, `world_generation_contract_test` and `terrain_recipe_freeze_test`, each with a `--golden=` can-fail check. Earlier rows must stay byte-identical. The primary runs `-Suite gate` at the commit point. Two groups accepted in the same review may share one step (plan 2.2).
