## What T8 delivers

Three things the user asked for:

1. **A rock face texture for cliffs** (track R). Today's "alpine rock" is loose gravel; it makes a cliff look like a gravel wall (T4 Q1). T8 adds one CC0 rock face set with recorded provenance. The gravel stays where gravel belongs (the alpine biome floor).
2. **Dense canopies on the most common trees** (track S, form D, "dominant broadleaf"). Closed stands cover 37 % of the ground with crown hulls (24 % by leaf opacity) at about 49 canopy stems per hectare. More stems are too expensive (1.46x stems cost +1.2 ms). The lever left is **cover per stem**: wide, well-filled crowns on the trees that fill the stand.
3. **A tall, dense conifer** (track S, form C). Firs are dense (top fill 0.68-0.75) but at most 9.3 m. Pines reach 32 m but are open (top fill 0.43-0.58). The new form is a 20-35 m fir or spruce with the fir's fill.

## Design decisions made by this plan

T8-R1, T8-S1 and T8-S2 fill in the detail. Later WOs cite these by number.

**Track R**

- R-D1. **A separate rock uniform; the gravel stays.** New uniforms `ground_rock_albedo` and `ground_rock_normal` are read only by the ground variants (F18) and the scatter rock shader. `biome_alpine_albedo` keeps its file and stays the alpine biome layer in every generation. Generation 1 never binds or samples the new set.
- R-D2. **One set in the first pass:** a massive, jointed rock face, tileable, with no strong bedding (T4's procedural strata supply the layering), no baked light and no moss. A second, layered set is a later option, only if the review asks.
- R-D3. **No roughness texture in the first pass** (F16). It costs a second triplanar tap set for a surface that is rough everywhere. Revisit only if wet rock looks wrong.
- R-D4. The authored normal map replaces the generated detail normal behind the same global. `tools/GroundRockDetailNormal.cs` and its test are retired for the rock (the unknown-origin derivative then leaves the ground variants).
- R-D5. It lands **before** T4-8 under `ground.surface` and `scatter.rocks` (T4 design "Swap path"); no new feature bit.
- R-D6. **Salt crust** (T4 answer R1, "comes with T8's textures") stays deferred. It needs a per-vertex carrier on every tier, which T4 judged a format change for one material. It becomes its own later step if the user asks again.

**Track S**

- I1. **Feature.** `vegetation.new_species` (bit 13) joins the ledger as an open feature. Appended rows carry it as `EnablingFeature`. It stays separate from bit 6 so that the harness can compare the forest with and without the new trees in one build (`--feature-ab=`), and so that vegetation can still freeze alone if the user rejects the trees at G3. `tree_catalog_append_test` moves its synthetic feature to an unused name.
- I2. **Catalog.** A third catalog, `SpeciesTreeCatalog` (`TreeSourceCatalog.T8Species = 2`), with its own prepared resource, manifest and tool, on the `DerivedTreeCatalog` pattern (F4). Indices start at 55. The base catalog, its pipeline and its 30 are not touched (A6a).
- I3. **No fourth species. The new forms take existing habitat roles.**
  - The tall conifer (form C) is filed under `TreeSpecies.Fir` as its upper size classes: it is the mature fir-spruce stand, which is also where the user placed it (F14). Young fir stands keep the 4.2 m and 9.3 m firs, which become the sub-canopy.
  - The dominant broadleaf (form D) is filed under `TreeSpecies.Maple` as additional medium and large canopy variants.
  - Per-form differences (trunk fractions, material quirks, porosity) ride on the row's `Import` record and the catalog manifest, not on the enum.
  - Reason: every three-way site in F11-F13 stays as it is. A fourth role is needed only by the deferred T8b forms (riparian, disturbance).
- I4. **Routes, in order of preference per form** (user answer 1):
  - **A. Derived in-repo from the LOLIPOP parts** (the T6 derived-catalog precedent). Form C: a generated tapered trunk with whorls of bough cards from the fir bough atlas and fir bark, at true scale. Form D: maple crowns re-composed from the maple cluster cards on the large maple's branch structure, for a wider and fuller crown at fewer triangles. No download, no new licence, no new atlas in VRAM, a growth series that matches the existing young trees.
  - **B. A CC0 or CC-BY pack**, if one fits the import contract (F2) with three size steps.
  - **C. An offline generator** (Blender with a tree generator, textures from CC0 atlases). Blender is not installed on the 4070 desktop today; the user allowed installing it when a form needs it (2026-11-21). Install it only then, and say so in the hand-back.
  - All three routes are approved, in this order (user, 2026-11-21). The user picks per form from contact sheets and measured numbers. Runtime generation stays rejected (plan 7.2).
- I5. **Far tiers.** Appended canopy variants get the second `ImportedTreeImpostorCatalog` instance A6a describes: own shared mesh, own arrays, own bake and calibration files, own far group set decoding `INSTANCE_CUSTOM.x - 55`. They share the slot caps of the base set (B10).
- I6. **Placement.** `ForestStructureSampler` gains version 3 tables used only when the world has bit 13: fir gains the new upper classes; maple's medium and large classes draw the form D variants with a weight T8-S7 calibrates against F1-F3. `TreeSizeClass` and `AgeRank` are appended, never renumbered. Worlds without bit 13 keep version 2 exactly.

- **The renderer generalisation (T8-S5a) is the largest item.** Thirty is assumed in about fifteen places (F7). The base-only identity tests are the guard.

- **Unknown-origin textures remain** for the forest, tundra, desert and alpine biome floors (F17). They are part of generation 1 and cannot be swapped there. Replacing them for new generations is not in T8; the primary should raise it before any build is distributed.
