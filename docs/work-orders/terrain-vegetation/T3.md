## Phase facts

Verified by reading the code on branch `gpu-offload`, 2026-11-10. Line numbers drift; re-check before editing. T2.md F1-F31 also apply.

**Player motion**

- H1. `RingVoxelExplorerController` is a `CharacterBody3D` (scripts/Voxel/Integration/RingVoxelExplorerController.cs:15). It keeps an authoritative canonical position and velocity (`_canonicalPosition`, `_canonicalVelocity`, where X is around, Y is vertical and Z is across).
- H2. The physics step calls `UpdateWaterState` and then `ProcessSwimming` while swimming (:971-977). In noclip, swimming is forced off (:962-968).
- H3. Walking sets the planar velocity with `MoveToward(desired * speed, acceleration * dt)` (:992-995).
- H4. Swimming (`ProcessSwimming`, :1344-1388):
  - The target velocity is input only, `desiredDirection * horizontalSpeed` (:1379-1381), approached at `SwimAcceleration` (:1386-1388).
  - The float target is `CurrentWaterSurfaceHeightMeters - SwimSurfaceFloatDepthMeters` (:1371).
  - The constants (:74-81) are swim 5.5 m/s, sprint 8.5 m/s, acceleration 12, float depth 1.15 m, entry depth 0.55 m and exit depth 0.15 m.
  - `MaxWalkableSlopeDegrees` is 55 (:30), and the user can change it (`SetMaxWalkableSlopeDegrees`, :248-253).
- H5. Terrain3D movement (RingVoxelExplorerController.SurfaceTerrain.cs:42-82) converts the canonical velocity to world space:
  - the tangent is the around direction, up is radial (minus `radialOut`), and **across is world +Y** (:61-65);
  - the move is `MoveAndSlide()` (:66);
  - collision exists only inside coverage (`EnsureCoverage` and `HasCollisionCoverage`, :46-58); the height query is `SampleCollisionHeight` (:29).
  `ApplyWorldTransform` uses the same frame (RingVoxelExplorerController.cs:2895-2926, across = `Vector3.Up` at :2903).

**Floating origin**

- H6. The collision origin recentres when the player leaves the current half-sector, with `CollisionSectorSizeMeters = 32768` (:18, :2956-2977). It then calls `VoxelChunkGridManager.SetFloatingOriginCanonicalAnchor` (scripts/Voxel/Phase2/VoxelChunkGridManager.cs:296-336). That method keeps the anchor on a continuous arc across the seam (:307-320) and raises `CanonicalFloatingOriginAnchorChanged(previousGeometry, currentGeometry, anchor)` (:293-294, :332-335). Subscribers reposition their nodes, for example RingTerrainProxySector.cs:882, RingTerrain3DGameplay.cs:236 and scripts/poi/Emitters/EmitterPoiStreamer.cs:179.
- H7. No `RigidBody3D` exists in scripts/ or scenes/ (grep, 0 files). project.godot has no `physics` section, so the 3D physics engine and the default gravity are Godot defaults. Godot's default gravity points along -Y. In this world that is the across direction (H5), so a default rigid body would fall sideways across the ring.

**Water ambience**

- H8. The controller probes the raw sampler every 0.4 s at 48, 128, 320 and 640 m in 8 directions (:1260-1335). It publishes the kind, proximity and shore proximity as meta (:1337-1342).
- H9. scripts/water_ambience.gd:56-74 maps kind 3 (River) to a "river" layer whose level is proximity only; there is no speed term.
- H10. The `River` player in scenes/VoxelRingIntegrationTest.tscn:246-249 streams `res://resources/audio/water/shore_wave_cc0.ogg` (ext_resource :66) at pitch 1.22. resources/audio/water/ holds only that file and LICENSE.md. Legacy gen-16 rivers use this player today (T2.md F19).

**Water shader and proxy water mesh**

- H11. River advection is a scalar along +x (materials/ring_water_common.gdshaderinc:404, :439), fed by `water_river_advection` (:10) and the globals at :20-21 and :61-63. The river profile scales are at :166-171.
- H12. The CPU water vertices use COLOR (colour plus the kind in alpha), UV, and UV2 for morph (RingTerrainProxySector.cs:3882-3899). The compact water arrays carry only vertices, normals, colours, UV and UV2 (RingTerrainProxySector.VertexTable.cs:693-719). A per-vertex velocity therefore needs a new channel in both the mesh path and the compact path. Inland water never uses the shared ocean grid (:657).

## Work orders

### T3-1. Current, danger, coupling, visuals and audio design

ROLE: sol_lead (design)
SIZE: M
DEPENDS ON: T2-2, T2-5

OBJECTIVE
Write design sections E1-E10 of this file: the danger calibration, the flow-field rules and constants, the downstream-exit and softening rules, exclusions, swim and wade coupling, the `RingWaterFlow` API, the per-vertex flow attribute and shader advection, foam, ambience and whitewater mappings, the current strength setting, and the chute rock hook. T3-3 to T3-11 then need no further design choices.

CONTEXT / ROOT CAUSE
- T0 found that no single constant reaches the danger targets (H14): at least 70 % calm or swift, 5-10 % fierce, at most 3 % torrent, by length.
- The T0 spec says the targets need "a steeper-than-linear mapping (for example the plan's river ferocity scaling of n and the caps), or a gradient baseline longer than 5 km", and leaves that to design.
- T2's breaching changes the gradients and T2 models confinement (which raises speeds), so the calibration must use the production graph.

FILES/SYMBOLS
- This file, E1-E10.
- A calibration probe may live in tools/ (for example a `--calibrate` mode of `drainage_bake_benchmark`, or a small manual tool). It reads the T2 graph and changes no production behaviour.

REQUIRED CHANGES
1. **E1, calibration.** On the T2 production graph, for seed 1337 and seed 4242, measure the class shares by length for these candidates:
   - (a) gradient baselines of 5, 10 and 20 km, and a reach-averaged gradient (the drop over the reach length, with a minimum length);
   - (b) a compression of steep gradients, S_eff = S_ref (S / S_ref)^gamma with gamma < 1, where `river_ferocity` sets gamma and scales the caps;
   - (c) `river_ferocity` scaling n;
   - (d) combinations of these, with confinement on.
   Choose the simplest rule that meets all three targets on both seeds. Define the `river_ferocity` recipe field: its semantics, range and the default that meets the targets. Record the table.
2. **E2, flow rules** (plan 3.2 table):
   - direction;
   - along the reach: the pool speed U, and a chute factor up to 3 times, capped;
   - across the channel: parabolic, 1.5 U at the centre;
   - with depth: 1.0 at the surface to 0.6 at the bed;
   - lakes: an outlet draw within 100 m and a decaying inlet jet;
   - the estuary fade;
   - no ocean flow;
   - caps: 10 m/s in pools and 12 m/s in chutes, times the current strength;
   - every constant, with continuity at reach joins and confluences.
3. **E3, exit rule.** Every fierce or torrent reach must be followed within 5 km by a calm or swift reach with a walkable bank. Define "walkable bank": the bank slope at or below a fixed 35 degrees, not the user-adjustable `MaxWalkableSlopeDegrees`, so the rule does not depend on preferences. Define the **softening** applied where the rule fails (T0: about 44 % of dangerous cells fail at 5 km). Candidates: insert a calm pool with low banks, open the valley locally, or lower the class by raising roughness. Softening must keep Q = U w d and the T2 carve invariants.
4. **E4, exclusions.** No dangerous reach touches an emitter site, Wall Engine, the Weather Lung or the spawn area. Locate the spawn definition (V5) and state the radius.
5. **E5, coupling** (plan 3.4).
   - Swimming: target velocity = input + water velocity, using the existing `SwimAcceleration`.
   - Wading: when depth is below `SwimEnterDepthMeters`, add an acceleration toward the water velocity scaled by (depth / entry)^2.
   - Float: the target follows the surface down a chute.
   - The player is never pinned against the flow.
   - Noclip and flight are unaffected.
6. **E6, API.** Define `RingWaterFlow.Sample`: its signature, including the footprint argument that T2.md F31(a) and D7 require of every river query (0 for physics), its result struct, thread safety, the cost target (under 0.3 us), how it shares code with the carve so the bed and surface equal the sampler's (plan 3.7), and the per-frame caching in the controller.
7. **E7, visible flow.**
   - The vertex channel for velocity (V4; CUSTOM0 is proposed) in both the mesh path and the compact path (H12), and its encoding.
   - Two-phase flow-map advection that replaces the scalar x advection (H11) for River-kind water only, gated by the `river_current` bit.
   - Legacy gen-16 rivers keep the scalar path (T2.md F19).
   - The footprint fade beyond a few hundred metres.
8. **E8, mappings.** Monotone functions of speed (and discharge, for sound) for foam coverage, the ambience level and its audible radius (300-500 m for a torrent), and far whitewater. Plan 3.3 lists the cues.
9. **E9, current strength.** The live setting: key `current_strength`, range 0-2, default 1. Its owner is `GameplayWaterDefaults`. It publishes the shader global `ringworld_current_strength`, and the C# physics reads the same value. At 0, water is still everywhere, for physics and visuals alike.
10. **E10, chute rocks.** The data that T5's chute boulders read (position, chute index, strength, side), as a query on the graph. T3 renders nothing.

DO-NOT-CHANGE CONSTRAINTS
- Generation 1 worlds must stay identical: `world_generation_fingerprint_test` must pass unchanged.
- No production code changes.
- The class thresholds (1.5, 4 and 7 m/s centre pool speed) and the targets are fixed by the plan.

VALIDATION STEPS
- The E1 table covers both seeds. The primary agent reviews E1-E10 before T3-3.
- Any probe run follows godot-test-run, and `tools/prune_test_output.ps1 -Apply` runs at the end.
- Send the user the river map coloured by danger for the chosen calibration, with the T0 map beside it.

ESCALATION CONDITIONS
- No candidate meets all three targets on both seeds, or one class exceeds 90 % (the plan risk "The danger mix", a design stop). Report the closest candidates: the primary agent asks the user whether to move the targets.
- Softening cannot meet the exit rule without breaking T2's carve invariants.

### T3-4. `RingWaterFlow`: the single source of truth

ROLE: sol_implementer
SIZE: M
DEPENDS ON: T3-3, T2-9

OBJECTIVE
`RingWaterFlow.Sample(position)` returns the water kind, surface, bed, velocity, speed, reach id and danger class anywhere on the ring. It is a pure function of position and the resident graph. Its bed and surface equal the terrain sampler's, and it is fast enough for physics.

CONTEXT / ROOT CAUSE
Plan 3.2 ("One source of truth"), 3.7, E2 and E6. Physics, the water mesh, the far shader and audio all call it. Nothing reads flow from a mesh or a streamed chunk.

FILES/SYMBOLS
- New: scripts/Terrain/Hydrology/RingWaterFlow.cs and WaterFlowSample.cs.
- Shares the segment lookup and level code with `RiverCarveOperator` (T2-7), with no duplicated arithmetic.
- New: tools/RiverFlowFieldTest.cs and tools/river_flow_field_test.gd.

REQUIRED CHANGES
1. Implement E6's API and E2's rules: pools and chutes, the cross-channel and depth profiles, lake draw and inlet jets, the estuary fade, no ocean flow, and the caps times current strength (read from E9's owner; 1 until T3-5 lands).
2. Thread-safe and allocation-free. It returns "no water" for worlds without `river_current`, or when no graph exists.
3. Print the cost per call from a benchmark loop inside the test (target under 0.3 us).

DO-NOT-CHANGE CONSTRAINTS
- Generation 1 worlds must stay identical: `world_generation_fingerprint_test` must pass unchanged.
- No consumer is wired yet.

VALIDATION STEPS
1. New `river_flow_field_test` (headless, review world). It checks:
   - U w d = Q along 200 reaches within 2 %;
   - speed is 0 at the banks;
   - the velocity points downstream (dot product with the centreline tangent above 0);
   - there is no flow on land or in the ocean;

   - the bed and surface equal the terrain sampler's at 1,000 points within 0.01 m;
   - values are periodic across the seam and deterministic;
   - lake outlet draw and inlet jet are present;
   - the estuary fades to 0;
   - at current strength 0, every speed is 0.
   Negative control: a fabricated reach with mismatched width must fail the continuity check.
   Manifest entry: `"river_flow_field_test": {"kind": "test", "mode": "headless", "tier": "gate", "tags": ["water", "hydrology"], "marker": "RIVER_FLOW_FIELD", "expected_s": 40, "timeout_s": 180}`.

- the current carries a swimmer and shoves a wader;
- chutes dunk the swimmer;
- a calm river barely moves them, and a torrent sweeps them to the next calm reach.
The live setting "Current strength" scales physics and visuals together.

CONTEXT / ROOT CAUSE
Plan 3.4, 3.3 ("What dangerous means"), E5 and E9, and H2-H4. Dangerous water displaces the player and never harms them (plan 13.2 item 15, accepted).
