## Phase rules

The "Phase rules" of T2.md apply to every order here, in addition to TEMPLATE.md: Claude Code executors (`implementer`, `claude-lead`; `claude-deep` only with the primary's approval), production first, sampling with tolerances (99.5 % and a hard limit), a STOP RULE on every order, no new requirements from inside, the reduced testing cadence, at most five evidence links per report. Added for T3:

- **T3 does not move rivers.** No order changes a centreline, a level profile, a lake level or a lake cell. T3R-3 re-measures the straight-run criterion to show it.
- **A class is a measurement.** The class of a span comes from its danger speed, and a test compares that with the flow field.
- **Commit points** (the primary runs `-Suite gate` and commits): **C4** after T3R-3, **C5** after T3R-7, **C6** at T3R-12.

- **Constants** marked **(tune at review)** in the Spec are starting values from a scratch calibration, not measurements of production code.

### T3R-4. `RingWaterFlow`: the single source of truth

ROLE: implementer
SIZE: M
DEPENDS ON: T3R-3, T2R-8

OBJECTIVE
`RingWaterFlow.Sample` returns kind, surface, bed, velocity, speed, span, class, danger speed, whitewater and chute head anywhere on the ring, as a pure function of position, the resident graph and the current strength, fast enough for physics.

CONTEXT / ROOT CAUSE
Plan 3.2 and 3.7; reset doc 4.7; Spec E5. Physics, the water mesh, the far shader and audio all call it. Nothing reads flow from a mesh.

FILES/SYMBOLS
- New: scripts/Terrain/Hydrology/RingWaterFlow.cs, WaterFlowSample.cs, IWaterFlowSource.cs (if T3R-6 has not already added it).
- RiverCarveOperator.cs: expose the location method and the core bed static (reset doc 7, items 1 and 2) if T2R-7 did not. No arithmetic is copied.
- RiverLevel.cs: one method returning the displayed level and the chute head B at s.
- New: tools/RiverFlowFieldTest.cs and tools/river_flow_field_test.gd.

REQUIRED CHANGES
1. E5's API and rules: location, surface, bed, across and depth profiles, the chute boost, span blends, fade-in at sources and outlets, inlet jets, sea and dry-pan fades, caps, the current strength argument.
2. Thread-safe and allocation-free. Kind None without a graph or without the `water.current` feature.
3. Register `water.current` in the ledger as an open feature (bit 1), as T2R-2 registered `water.rivers`.
4. The test prints the cost per call inside the channel, in a lake and away from water.

DO-NOT-CHANGE CONSTRAINTS
- Generation 1 worlds must stay identical: `world_generation_fingerprint_test` must pass unchanged.
- No consumer is wired. The carve's output is unchanged (`river_carve_invariants_test`).

VALIDATION STEPS
1. New `river_flow_field_test` (headless, CPU strip graph). Checks, each at 99.5 % with its hard limit:
   - the mean across the channel of the pool speed times w times d equals Q within 2 % on 200 spans (hard 5 %);
   - speed is 0 at and beyond the channel edge; the velocity points downstream (dot product with the tangent above 0);
   - no flow on land, in the ocean, or in a lake more than 400 m from an inlet or outlet;
   - speeds within the caps; at current strength 0 every speed is 0; at 2 every speed doubles;
   - **the class is a measurement**: the mean centre speed sampled every 5 m along 200 spans equals the span's `Vd` within 15 % (hard 30 %);
   - surface and bed equal the terrain sampler's at 1,000 points in channels within 0.01 m (through the model with a strip graph);
   - continuity: a speed step between samples 1 m apart of at most 0.5 m/s (hard 2 m/s), confluence shear lines excluded and counted;
   - periodic across the seam; deterministic; an inlet jet and an outlet fade-in are present.
   Negative control: a fabricated span with its width doubled must fail the discharge check.
   Manifest: `"river_flow_field_test": {"kind": "test", "mode": "headless", "tier": "gate", "tags": ["water", "hydrology"], "marker": "RIVER_FLOW_FIELD", "expected_s": 40, "timeout_s": 180}`.
2. `.\tools\run_tests.ps1 -Tests river_flow_field_test,river_carve_invariants_test -Label rivers -DryRun`, then run. At the end: `world_generation_fingerprint_test,world_generation_contract_test`.

ESCALATION CONDITIONS
- A call in the channel costs more than 1.0 us.
- Bed equality needs a height-model evaluation inside the query.

## Spec

Written on 2026-11-11 with the reset, against `7a2c8ba`. The reasons are in docs/t3-current-reset.md section 4. Values marked **(tune at review)** are starting values. Notation as T2.md's Spec; `g = 9.81`; `fer` = `river_ferocity`.

### E1. Spans and records

- **Span**: a stretch of one reach. A reach of k channel cells has `ceil(k / 2)` spans of equal cell count (the last takes the remainder). Span boundaries are the arc lengths of those cells' nodes on the stem.
- **Stored** (`RiverSpan`, 24 B, sorted by reach then `SStart`): `Reach` (int32), `SStart`, `SEnd` (float), `Q` (float, discharge at the span's last cell), `Sh` (float), `Landing` (float: 1 = none, above 1 = the factor of E4).
- `Sh = max((W(s_m - x0) - W(s_m + x1)) / (x0 + x1), 1e-4)`, W = `RiverLevel.Stored`, `s_m` the span's middle, `x0 = min(2500, s_m)`, `x1 = min(2500, s_end - s_m)`. Computed after `RiverLevel.Build`.
- `Sr = max(0, (W(SStart) - W(SEnd)) / (SEnd - SStart))`, derived at load.
- **Derived at load** per span (floats): half width, bank width, depth, edge depth, floodplain width, valley slope, `Vp`, `eta`, `Vd`, whiteness, class. Per reach: `SpanStart`, `SpanCount`, and the **path section** (today's `RiverHydraulics.Section` of the reach's `Q` and `S`), which keeps feeding `MeanderAmplitude`, `MeanderWavelength`, `WanderAmplitude` and the bank-line station offset.
- **Blend**: channel and valley values blend from the upstream span over the first 200 m of a span (`smoothstep`), also across reach boundaries on a stem. The first span of a stem does not blend. Q and the class do not blend.
- Floodplain and valley slope use S6's formulas with the span's w and `Sh`. A landing, and the first calm or swift span after a dangerous one, limit the valley slope to 0.6.
- **Versions**: `GraphFormat` 4 (payload gains spans after reaches), `DrainageAlgorithm` 3, `CarveOperator` 2.
- **Bytes**: about 75,000 spans; +1.8 MB stored, about +3.6 MB resident; expect 30 MiB against the 32 MiB gate.
- **Checked by** `river_danger_test` and the graph report.

### E2. Hydraulics

Per span, in `RiverDanger.Channel`:
```
d   = max(1.2, channel_depth * Kd * Q^0.4)                       Kd = 0.34
n   = L * clamp(Np * (Sh / Sa)^Gn, Np, Nm) / fer                 Np = 0.05, Nm = 0.20, Sa = 0.0003, Gn = 0.4; L = the span's Landing
U0  = min(d^(2/3) * sqrt(Sh) / n, Vpool / 1.5 * fer)             Vpool = 9
w   = clamp(Q / (U0 * d), 8, Wmax)                               Wmax = 400; if Q / (U0 * d) > Wmax then d = Q / (U0 * Wmax)
U   = Q / (w * d)
```
All five constants **(tune at review; T3R-2 fixes them)**. `river_ferocity`: recipe field, default 1, range 0.6 to 1.5, frozen with the world. Bank width, edge depth, freeboard and incision are S6's.
**Checked by** `river_danger_test`: `Q = U w d` within 1e-6; floors; widths at most 400; monotone in Sh and in Q.

### E3. Danger speed and classes

```
Vp  = 1.5 * U
c   = min(1, Lc * Sr)                                            Lc = 10 (RiverLevel.ChuteMeters)
h   = max(1, Ld * Sr)                                            Ld = 30
eta = (E0 / L) * clamp(d / Dref, 0.5, 1.6)                       E0 = 0.65, Dref = 2.2
Vc  = max(Vp, min(sqrt(Vp^2 + 2 g eta h), Vchute * fer))         Vchute = 12
Vd  = (1 - c) * Vp + c * Vc
```
Class: calm `Vd < 1.5`, swift `< 4`, fierce `< 7`, torrent otherwise. Whiteness of a span: `wbar = smoothstep(2.5, 7, Vd)`. E0, Dref and Ld **(tune at review)**.

Table to be replaced by T3R-2 with production numbers for both seeds. Scratch, seed 1337, whole ring (H21): calm 39.4 %, swift 49.1 %, fierce 10.0 %, torrent 1.5 % before landings; 39.4 / 50.6 / 8.8 / 1.3 % after. By ferocity (before landings): 0.6 gives 60.9 / 31.9 / 6.4 / 0.8; 0.8 gives 50.5 / 40.5 / 7.9 / 1.1; 1.25 gives 25.5 / 58.2 / 14.0 / 2.3; 1.5 gives 14.2 / 63.3 / 18.9 / 3.5.

Acceptance window for the constants (both seeds, after landings): calm at least 30 %, calm plus swift at least 85 %, fierce 6 to 9 %, torrent 1 to 2.5 %.
**Checked by** `river_danger_test` (bands, monotonicity) and `river_flow_field_test` (the mean centre speed against `Vd`).

### E4. Exit rule, landings and exclusions

- **Order.** Spans are visited upstream to downstream (stems in the builder's topological order). `D_in(span)` = the largest `D_out` among its predecessors: the previous span on the stem and the last span of every stem that joins inside it.
- **Dangerous** = fierce or torrent with `Landing = 1`. `D_out = D_in + length` for a dangerous span, else 0.
- **Landing.** A dangerous span with `D_in > 0` and `D_in + length > 5,000 m` becomes a landing: `Landing` = the smallest factor L in [1, 64] with `Vd(L) <= 3.0` (bisection, 40 steps; L multiplies n and divides eta). Then `D_out = 0`.
- **Exclusions.** A span that would be dangerous and has any point of its base curve within 1,500 m of an emitter site disc, the Wall Engine stadium or the Weather Lung footprint, or within 3,000 m of the nominal fresh-spawn position, is a landing. The nominal position is the main site's Far viewpoint target before the land check (H20), from a pure static added beside `TryComputeFreshSpawn`.
- **Exit valley.** Landings and the first calm or swift span after a dangerous one have their valley slope limited to 0.6.
- **Getting out of the water** needs no rule: the S7 bank and floodplain exist on every span. `river_current_swim_test` checks it in play.
- Expected (scratch, seed 1337): about 4,400 dangerous runs, 87 % of dangerous length already inside the rule, about 1,050 landings, 1.5 % of river length. Above 4 % is an escalation.
- **Checked by** `river_danger_test`.

### E5. `RingWaterFlow`

```
public readonly record struct WaterFlowSample(InlandWaterKind Kind, double Surface, double Bed, double VelocityAround, double VelocityAcross,
    double Speed, int Span, RiverClass Class, double DangerSpeed, double Whitewater, double ChuteHead);
public interface IWaterFlowSource { WaterFlowSample Sample(double around, double across, double height); }
public sealed class RingWaterFlow : IWaterFlowSource   // RingWaterFlow(RiverGraph graph, RiverCarveOperator carve); double CurrentStrength { get; set; }
```
`height` NaN means the surface. Kind None gives NaN levels and zero velocity. Thread-safe, allocation-free. Cost: target 0.5 us, gate 1.0 us in the channel; the `Near` bit exits first elsewhere.

- **River** (the carve's owner piece, `|t| < a + b`): `Surface = RiverLevel.AtStem(stem, s)`; `Bed` = the S7 core formula at t; direction = the displaced centreline's tangent, downstream.
- Chute head: `sigma(s) = clamp(L(s - 5) - L(s + 5), 0, 1)`, `B(s) = sigma(s) * max(1, L(s - Ld) - L(s))`, L the displayed level, arguments clamped to the stem.
- Centre speed: `vc(s) = min(sqrt(Vp^2 + 2 g eta B(s)), Vchute * fer)`, with Vp and eta blended as E1.
- Across: `vc * (1 - (t / a)^2)` for `|t| < a`, else 0. With depth: times `lerp(0.6, 1, clamp((height - Bed) / (Surface - Bed), 0, 1))`.
- Fade-in: times `smoothstep(0, D, s)` with `D = 200` at a stem head, `D = max(100, OutletS)` on a lake outlet stem.
- Fade-out: times `smoothstep(0, 500, s_end - s)` on stems ending in the sea or Dead. No fade before a lake or a confluence.
- **Inlet jet** (Lake kind, the stem end within 5 w): with x the distance beyond the end along the end tangent and y the lateral distance, speed `vc(s_end) * exp(-x / (3 w)) * exp(-(y / (a + 0.2 x))^2)` along the tangent, for `x > 0`.
- **Lake** (T2R-8's membership, P > 0, no river owner): `Surface = Level`, `Bed = NaN`, velocity 0 apart from a jet. The caller decides wetness from its own ground height.
- `Whitewater = max(smoothstep(2.5, 7, Speed / CurrentStrength), min(1, B))` in a river, 0 elsewhere (0 when the strength is 0). `ChuteHead = B`.
- Every velocity is multiplied by `CurrentStrength`.
- **Checked by** `river_flow_field_test`.

### E6. Coupling

- Swimming (`ProcessSwimming`): target = input velocity + (VelocityAround, VelocityAcross) on canonical X and Z, approached at `SwimAcceleration`. Float target = `Surface - SwimSurfaceFloatDepthMeters - min(0.6, 0.3 * ChuteHead)` **(tune at G2)**.
- Wading (depth above 0 and below the swim entry depth): add `WadeAcceleration * (depth / entry)^2 * (waterVelocity - planarVelocity)` to the walking step, `WadeAcceleration = 6` per second **(tune at G2)**.
- One sample per physics frame, only in or within 3 m of inland water. None in noclip, in flight, in the ocean or without `water.current`.
- **Checked by** `river_current_swim_test`.

### E7. Vertex flow and shader

- Channel: `CUSTOM0` as three floats (V1): velocity along the mesh's two horizontal axes in m/s at strength 1, and whiteness. Written for River and Lake vertices in the mesh path and the compact path; the v12 cache record stores them if the mesh is rebuilt from the cache.
- Shader, River kind with the `water.current` bit: two phases `p_i = fract(TIME / T + i / 2)`, `T = 3 s` **(tune at review)**, offsets `-velocity * ringworld_current_strength * p_i * T` in world metres applied to the normal and foam lookups, blended by `1 - abs(2 p_i - 1)`. Foam coverage = whiteness. The animation fades between pixel footprints of 1 m and 4 m; foam coverage stays.
- Proxy levels of 16 m and coarser carry whiteness from the span (`wbar`), not from the chute head, so chutes do not alias.
- **Checked by** `river_flow_visual_parity_test`.

### E8. Roar and far whitewater

- Roar of a span: `roar = wbar * clamp(sqrt(w / 40), 0.5, 1.5)`. Audible radius `R = 100 + 400 * min(1, roar)` m. Level at distance x from the nearest point of the span: `min(1, roar) * (1 - smoothstep(0.3 R, R, x))`. The controller takes the maximum over spans within 640 m and publishes `river_roar` and `river_roar_distance`. The calm layer keeps today's proximity rule and fades out as the roar rises above 0.5.
- Far whitewater of a cell: the sum over pieces of `length * w * wbar` divided by the sum of `length * w`, through T2R-12's whitewater hook, with its footprint rule.
- **Checked by** `river_ambience_test` and `river_far_water_fraction_test`.

### E9. Current strength

Key `current_strength`, range 0 to 2, default 1, Water tab, owner `GameplayWaterDefaults`. It sets `RingWaterFlow.CurrentStrength` and the shader global `ringworld_current_strength`. It does not change the class, the geometry or the roar. **Checked by** `water_settings_smoke_test`.

### E10. Chute query

`readonly record struct RiverChute(double Around, double Across, int Span, float Head, float HalfWidth, float TangentAround, float TangentAcross)`. A chute is a whole-metre crossing of the stored profile; its position is the crossing (the chute's downstream end) on the displaced centreline; `Head` is `B` there. Enumerated for the pieces of the buckets that overlap the rectangle, ordered by (stem, s). **Checked by** `river_chute_hook_test`.

### F1. Backend

**Decision.** A canonical integrator. No `RigidBody3D`, no physics-server gravity, no project physics settings. The body's node may be an `AnimatableBody3D` so the player can touch it. Reasons: reset doc 4.9. If the user answers question 4 otherwise, T3R-6 stops and the lead writes a probe order.

### F2. `RingBuoyancy`

State: canonical position (around, across, height), velocity, yaw. One step of `dt` with a flow sample and the ground height:
- Vertical: a critically damped spring toward `Surface - draft`, natural period 1.2 s **(tune at review)**. Never below `ground + 0.05`.
- Horizontal: `v += (waterVelocity - v) * (1 - exp(-dt * drag))`, `drag` default `1 / 1.5` per second.
- Stranding: with `depth = Surface - ground` under `draft`, a deceleration of `3 * (1 - depth / draft)` m/s^2 against the horizontal velocity. With `may_strand` off, a push of 0.5 m/s toward deeper water (the flow sample's channel offset) instead.
- Out of water: velocity to 0 over 0.5 s, resting on the ground.
- Yaw turns toward the flow direction at up to 0.5 rad/s when the water speed is above 0.3 m/s. Pitch follows the surface slope along the flow.
- Steps longer than 1/30 s are split.
**Checked by** `floating_body_test`.

### F3. `RingFloatingBody`

Exports: `mass` (kg, for later contact use), `draft` (m; default 40 % of the collision shape's height), `drag`, `sample_points` (1 to 4; the flow is averaged), `may_strand`. Sleep after 3 s under 0.05 m/s; a sleeping body takes no sample; it wakes on `Wake()` or when the manager's check (one sample every 2 s) finds water faster than 0.3 m/s at it. The node transform is written every physics frame from the canonical state through `RingLodGeometrySnapshot.CanonicalToWorld`, so an anchor change needs no handler. **Checked by** `floating_body_test` and `floating_item_validation_test`.

### F4. Manager and catalog

Radius 300 m; at most 32 awake bodies, nearest first; the rest sleep in place and are hidden beyond 600 m; nothing is saved. Cost gate 0.1 ms at the cap. Catalog row: scene path, spawn rule, density, allowed classes; no rows ship. **Checked by** `floating_item_validation_test`.
