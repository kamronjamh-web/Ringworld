- P5. LOD0-LOD2 of every variant have two surfaces, bark then foliage; **LOD3 is a single billboard surface in all 30**, and the impostor packer throws on more than one (`ImportedTreeImpostorCatalog.cs:55`). Bark-only forms (snags, fallen trees, stumps) can come from LOD0-2 only and need their own far treatment.
- P6. The far impostor stores every variant at 256 x 256 (`TextureLayerSize`, `ImportedTreeImpostorCatalog.cs:11`), 4-8x below the authored billboards. It is shared by every generation, so changing it changes generation 1. Out of scope.
- P7. Pine LOD3 lacks metallic and roughness maps (T0 8.4 finding 3). **Verified harmless:** the packer's fallback metallic image has blue = 1 (`ImportedTreeImpostorCatalog.cs:180`), but the impostor multiplies it by the material's `metallic_factor` (`imported_tree_impostor.gdshader:127`, parameter row from `ImportedTreeImpostorCatalog.cs:232`). The pine billboard materials have `metallicFactor` 0.0 in `resources/trees/lolipop/pine/scene.gltf`. Far pines are therefore not metallic; roughness is the fallback 1 times `roughnessFactor` 0.78-0.88. No action.
- P8. Leaf-cluster and bark textures for shrubs and dead wood, under `resources/trees/lolipop/<species>/textures/` (all CC-BY-4.0; derivatives carry the credit with a "modified" note, plan 7.3, `THIRD_PARTY_ASSETS.md:48-65`):
  - maple: `Cluster_Mat_{baseColor,normal,metallicRoughness}.png`;
  - pine: `Clusters_Mat_*`;
  - fir: `Brunches_Mat_*`;
  - `Bark_Mat_*` in each.
- P9. LOD switch (T0 8.6). With the user's prefs (near-detail fade 25-200 m) there was no visible LOD0-to-LOD2 pop from 15 to 60 m. The 60-200 m tail and the shipped defaults (Medium 20-30 m, `RingGraphicsQualityController.cs:270-277`) are untested. LOD1 is not drawn by the compact profile.

### Grass cards (default style)

- P13. Card encoding and atlas:
  - kinds are `Card = 0`, `Low = 1`, and 2 is retired and must never be reused (`scripts/Voxel/LOD/RingGrassCardPlacement.cs:6`); `AtlasVariants = 4` (11);
  - card bounds: `BoundsHorizontalExtent` 2.0 and `BoundsHeightScale` 2.5 (30-32); `CardFootprintMeters` 0.7 (`RingGrassStyleProfile.cs:20`).
  - The atlas is procedural: 2048 x 512, 4 variants, R coverage, G height gradient, B per-blade random, A constant 128 (`RingGrassCardAtlasGenerator.cs:69-81`, A at 190); `GeneratorVersion = 2` (77).
- P14. Card shader decode (`materials/ring_grass_cards.gdshader:84-89`):
  - `kind = floor(kind_slot * 0.25)`, `variant = kind_slot - kind*4`, and **`low = kind > 0.5`**, so a new kind 3 or higher would be drawn as the low layer unless the decode changes. Generation 1 only emits kinds 0 and 1, so a decode change that keeps 0 and 1 identical is output-neutral.
  - The atlas UV is `(variant + UV.x) * 0.25` (159).
- P15. Card builder (`RingGrassTileBuilder.cs`):
  - placement chance comes from the biome weights, with forest weight at 0.42 (241-247), so **any change to biome weights changes grass density**;
  - the variant hash and the `CUSTOM` packing are at 317-323.
  - The placement fingerprint includes the atlas parameters (`RingGrassStyleProfile.cs:127`), and the fingerprint golden pins every card argument (`WorldGenerationFingerprint.cs:118-144`). **The generation 1 atlas and placement values must not change**; new kinds use a second atlas.

### Cost, re-checked against T0 8.6

- C1. Plan gates (A1000, Medium, forest-edge pose): 0.3 ms GPU; 16 draws; at most 3,000 shrubs and 400 fallen trees resident; ground cover budget-neutral; appended variants 8 MB.
- C2. **The 0.3 ms gate does not fit the tree changes.** On the 4070 trees cost 8.6-10.7 ms at the forest edge and interior (about 60 % of root GPU) and 5.4 ms at the clearing, with 13-16 million tree primitives.
  - LOD0 triangle counts span 500 (sapling) to 22,500 (large maple), and the user's prefs keep LOD0 dithering out to 200 m (P9).
  - Taking saplings out of the canopy and choosing size by stand age will move tree cost by more than 0.3 ms, in either direction.
  - The plan set the T6 budget assuming trees cost less (handoff, "Cost").
- C3. **Re-scoped gates (for the user to confirm; see the parent report):**
  - (a) new classes (understory saplings, dead wood, shrubs): at most 0.3 ms on the A1000 and 16 draws, measured by a new hide class `understory_hidden`, same-boot A-B-A;
  - (b) canopy remix: the `trees_hidden` delta in the review world within +5 % of the generation 1 world at `forest_interior_eye`, `forest_edge_eye` and `forest_clearing_eye`, with `--prefs=user` and `--prefs=defaults`. Measured as alternating boots (generation 1, review, generation 1), two rounds each, with the cross-boot spread reported;
  - (c) ground cover: the `grass_hidden` delta within +0.05 ms of generation 1 (budget-neutral);
  - (d) far canopy: the far-viewport delta at most 0.05 ms.
  - Variant memory under 8 MB (C1).
- C4. **How T6 measures cost:** the T4-2 harness (`--world=`, `--feature-ab=` with tile rebuild for placement features) and T5-10's hide-class pattern. The A1000 is authoritative and the 4070 indicative.
