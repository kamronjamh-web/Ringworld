- P5. Bark-only forms (snags, fallen trees, stumps) can come from LOD0-2 only and need their own far treatment.
- P6. The far impostor stores every variant at 256 x 256 (`TextureLayerSize`, `ImportedTreeImpostorCatalog.cs:11`), 4-8x below the authored billboards. It is shared by every generation, so changing it changes generation 1. Out of scope.
- P7. Pine LOD3 lacks metallic and roughness maps (T0 8.4 finding 3). **Verified harmless:** the packer's fallback metallic image has blue = 1 (`ImportedTreeImpostorCatalog.cs:180`), but the impostor multiplies it by the material's `metallic_factor` (`imported_tree_impostor.gdshader:127`, parameter row from `ImportedTreeImpostorCatalog.cs:232`). The pine billboard materials have `metallicFactor` 0.0 in `resources/trees/lolipop/pine/scene.gltf`. Far pines are therefore not metallic; roughness is the fallback 1 times `roughnessFactor` 0.78-0.88. No action.
- P8. Leaf-cluster and bark textures for shrubs and dead wood, under `resources/trees/lolipop/<species>/textures/` (all CC-BY-4.0; derivatives carry the credit with a "modified" note, plan 7.3, `THIRD_PARTY_ASSETS.md:48-65`):

  - pine: `Clusters_Mat_*`;
  - fir: `Brunches_Mat_*`;
  - `Bark_Mat_*` in each.
- P9. LOD switch (T0 8.6). With the user's prefs (near-detail fade 25-200 m) there was no visible LOD0-to-LOD2 pop from 15 to 60 m. The 60-200 m tail and the shipped defaults (Medium 20-30 m, `RingGraphicsQualityController.cs:270-277`) are untested. LOD1 is not drawn by the compact profile.

### Grass cards (default style)

- P13. Card encoding and atlas:
  - kinds are `Card = 0`, `Low = 1`, and 2 is retired and must never be reused (`scripts/Voxel/LOD/RingGrassCardPlacement.cs:6`); `AtlasVariants = 4` (11);
  - card bounds: `BoundsHorizontalExtent` 2.0 and `BoundsHeightScale` 2.5 (30-32); `CardFootprintMeters` 0.7 (`RingGrassStyleProfile.cs:20`).
  - The atlas is procedural: 2048 x 512, 4 variants, R coverage, G height gradient, B per-blade random, A constant 128 (`RingGrassCardAtlasGenerator.cs:69-81`, A at 190); `GeneratorVersion = 2` (77).
- P14. Card shader decode (`materials/ring_grass_cards.gdshader:84-89`):
  - `kind = floor(kind_slot * 0.25)`, `variant = kind_slot - kind*4`, and **`low = kind > 0.5`**, so a new kind 3 or higher would be drawn as the low layer unless the decode changes. Generation 1 only emits kinds 0 and 1, so a decode change that keeps 0 and 1 identical is output-neutral.
  - The atlas UV is `(variant + UV.x) * 0.25` (159).
- P15. Card builder (`RingGrassTileBuilder.cs`):
  - placement chance comes from the biome weights, with forest weight at 0.42 (241-247), so **any change to biome weights changes grass density**;
  - the variant hash and the `CUSTOM` packing are at 317-323.
  - The placement fingerprint includes the atlas parameters (`RingGrassStyleProfile.cs:127`), and the fingerprint golden pins every card argument (`WorldGenerationFingerprint.cs:118-144`). **The generation 1 atlas and placement values must not change**; new kinds use a second atlas.

### Cost, re-checked against T0 8.6

- C1. Plan gates (A1000, Medium, forest-edge pose): 0.3 ms GPU; 16 draws; at most 3,000 shrubs and 400 fallen trees resident; ground cover budget-neutral; appended variants 8 MB.
- C2. **The 0.3 ms gate does not fit the tree changes.** On the 4070 trees cost 8.6-10.7 ms at the forest edge and interior (about 60 % of root GPU) and 5.4 ms at the clearing, with 13-16 million tree primitives.
  - LOD0 triangle counts span 500 (sapling) to 22,500 (large maple), and the user's prefs keep LOD0 dithering out to 200 m (P9).
  - Taking saplings out of the canopy and choosing size by stand age will move tree cost by more than 0.3 ms, in either direction.
  - The plan set the T6 budget assuming trees cost less (handoff, "Cost").
- C3. **Re-scoped gates (for the user to confirm; see the parent report):**
  - (a) new classes (understory saplings, dead wood, shrubs): at most 0.3 ms on the A1000 and 16 draws, measured by a new hide class `understory_hidden`, same-boot A-B-A;
  - (b) canopy remix: the `trees_hidden` delta in the review world within +5 % of the generation 1 world at `forest_interior_eye`, `forest_edge_eye` and `forest_clearing_eye`, with `--prefs=user` and `--prefs=defaults`. Measured as alternating boots (generation 1, review, generation 1), two rounds each, with the cross-boot spread reported;
  - (c) ground cover: the `grass_hidden` delta within +0.05 ms of generation 1 (budget-neutral);
  - (d) far canopy: the far-viewport delta at most 0.05 ms.
  - Variant memory under 8 MB (C1).
- C4. **How T6 measures cost:** the T4-2 harness (`--world=`, `--feature-ab=` with tile rebuild for placement features) and T5-10's hide-class pattern. The A1000 is authoritative and the 4070 indicative.

## T6 design A: forest structure, communities, derived forms, tint and cost

T6-1, claude-lead, 2026-11-19. Written against `6e33109` plus the working tree (T5 implemented through T5-10; forest lighting on by default). Numbers marked **(probe)** come from one headless CPU probe on the RTX 4070 desktop (single thread, no fill load, `LandscapeWorld.Default` seed 1337, production tile layout). ### Summary A

- **One source table.** `ImportedTreeSourceTable` lists all 30 base variants plus 25 derived forms (indices 30-54). The base catalog, its prepared resource, the impostor and the generation 1 code paths are not edited.
- **Canopy remix in the tree tile build, behind bit 6.**
  - Stand age (about 600 m patches) chooses the size class per species.
  - Age also scales the candidate chance (young stands 1.35x, old stands 0.65x) and the spacing radius.
  - Species come from habitat fields, with the old pockets kept as 25 % of the weight. A treeline thins and dwarfs trees by temperature.
  - Saplings leave the canopy.
  - The selection order of generation 1 is kept: spacing first, then species, then class. The habitat fields are therefore needed only at accepted trees.
- **New near classes are T5 scatter rows**, built by the existing streamer and stage jobs: snags, shrubs, saplings, fallen trees and stumps. They render through a second instance of the scatter layer pattern, `UnderstoryLayer`.
  - The layer owns its own compute instance and draws vertex-fetch carriers with species texture arrays.
  - That is **6 camera outputs plus 1 caster: at most 14 draws** at Medium by worst-case counting.
- **Sightlines.** In closed broadleaf the densities are about 30 saplings and 200 shrubs per hectare. Together with the trees this gives an eye-level free path of about **44 m**, inside the user's 30-60 m. Edge belts reach about 16 m; conifer stands stay open.
- **Tree primitives** at the three forest poses, from the planned size mix on the real generation 1 records (probe):
  - 40-300 m band (LOD2, most of the tree cost): **-6 %, +3 %, +4 %**.
  - Under 40 m (LOD0): -3 % at the interior, +30 % at the edge, but only 12 trees there.
  - Both are within the C3(b) +5 % gate where it matters, so the escalation condition (more than 10 %) is not triggered.
- **Cost risks.**
  - Tree tiles gain about 2-3 ms in feature worlds for the habitat fields.
  - Understory jobs cost about 15-40 ms per tile, inside about 500 m.
  - The A1000 0.3 ms GPU gate is likely missed. Questions 1 and 2 ask how both are read.

### A2. Stand age, size class, scale and spacing

**Sampler.** A new `scripts/Vegetation/ForestStructureSampler.cs` with `public const int AlgorithmVersion = 1` (2 since the v2 class table below). It holds a `ForestRegionSampler` (whose `Sample` and `AlgorithmVersion = 1` are untouched) and adds pure fields. Its value noise is a copy of `ForestRegionSampler.ValueNoise` and `Hash01`: same hash, same seam rule, its own salts.

- **Stand age:**
  - `raw = 0.7 * VN(around, across, 640 m, 0x5A6E1) + 0.3 * VN(192 m, 0x5A6E2)`;
  - `age = SmoothStep(0.22, 0.78, raw)`. The edges are the 5 % and 95 % quantiles of `raw` over 40,000 random land points. The resulting distribution by fifths is 25 / 16 / 16 / 16 / 27 %: patches of about 600 m, mostly young or old, with an internal 190 m texture.
- **Contrast:** `a_c = clamp(0.5 + (age - 0.5) * c)`, where `c` is the recipe field `forest_stand_age_contrast` (float, default 1.0, range 0-1.5; see A10 and Q3).
- **Effective age** (young edges and regrowth around clearings, dwarfing at the treeline):
  - `a = a_c * (0.5 + 0.5 SmoothStep(0.20, 0.30, density)) * (1 - 0.5 SmoothStep(0.05, 0.40, clearing)) * SmoothStep(0.24, 0.45, temperature)`;
  - `density` and `clearing` are the root's `ForestRegionSample`, `temperature` the root's `TerrainSample.Temperature`.
- **Size class per species.** A table at a = 0, 0.5 and 1, linear in between:

  | species | classes | a = 0 | a = 0.5 | a = 1 |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | maple | small, medium, large | .70 .25 .05 | .25 .50 .25 | .05 .45 .50 |
  | pine | small, medium, big, large | .60 .35 .05 0 | .20 .40 .25 .15 | .05 .25 .35 .35 |
  | fir | medium (4.2 m), large (9.3 m) | .9 .1 | .5 .5 | .25 .75 |

  **v2 (2026-11-20, user decision; `AlgorithmVersion = 2`).** The forest variant survey (`.godot/audit_baseline/common/forest/variant_survey/`) found the v1 table put the three small maples (6-7 m tall, 5 m crowns) at about 49 % of dense-forest stems, at about 50 stems per hectare, so dense stands covered only about 30 % of the ground. The canopy layer holds a mature-stand density, so v2 shifts every species about half a class up; small trees stay common in young stands and come back as saplings and shrubs (A7). Dense-forest maple small / medium / large went from .49 / .29 / .16 to .35 / .37 / .23, hull cover from 0.31 to 0.37 (seed 1337 probe). The v1 table was:

  | species | a = 0 | a = 0.5 | a = 1 | landscape mean (probe calc) |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | maple | .85 .15 0 | .45 .40 .15 | .15 .40 .45 | .475 .327 .197 |
  | pine | .75 .25 0 0 | .35 .35 .20 .10 | .10 .25 .30 .35 | .391 .291 .172 .147 |
  | fir | 1 0 | .6 .4 | .3 .7 | .625 .375 |

  The class is drawn with `Hash01(x, z, seed, salt + 8)`, and the variant inside the class with `salt + 4` (the generation 1 variant hash, reused). Inside a class the variants were uniform in v1; v2 draws the lighter mesh of a similar-fill pair twice as often (large maple 5, medium maples 7 and 8; `StructuredVariantWeight`), which offsets part of the triangle cost of the shift. v1 kept the mean triangles per tree at the generation 1 value (A10); v2 raises it (cost in the survey result).
- **Stems follow age:**
  - the candidate's chance becomes `ApplyGroveDensity(regional.Density * k(a) * tau_d(T), ...)`, with `k(a) = 1.35 - 0.7 a` (mean about 1 over the landscape; young thickets 1.35x, old stands self-thinned to 0.65x);
  - `tau_d` is the treeline term (A4);
  - the same `salt + 2` hash is compared, so a young stand accepts a superset of the generation 1 candidates.
  - The 12-candidate cell cap (133 per hectare) is **not** raised. Raising it would add tree build time and GPU roots linearly; the eye-level density comes from the understory instead (A7).
- **Spacing follows the age-expected size:** `ForestStructureSpacingMeters(moisture, density, a) = ForestLocalSpacingMeters(moisture, density) * (0.75 + 0.55 a)`. That is 1.9-6.8 m in young stands and 3.25-11.7 m in old ones.
  - It is a function of age, not of the drawn class, so the spacing competition runs before species and class, exactly as in generation 1.
  - Peers regenerate it from their own position, so the pass stays pure.
  - The 81 m² (9 m) pre-filter in `WinsForestSpacingCompetition` becomes the gated variant's maximum: 11.7 m, so 137 m².
- **Scale jitter** narrows to 0.92-1.08 (`salt + 6`), because the classes carry the variety.
- **Old stands also get snags.** Snags are a scatter row (A7), not a canopy form.
- **Code:** a new partial `RingBiomeDecorationLayer.ForestStructure.cs` with `SelectStructuredSpecies`, `SelectStructuredVariant`, `ForestStructureSpacingMeters`, a gated `WinsStructuredSpacingCompetition`, and `EvaluateSpacingCandidate` with the gated chance.
  - In `BuildVegetationTile` the only edit is one branch on `forestStructure = (request.Sampler.WorldFeatureMask & (1 << 6)) != 0`.
  - With the bit off, the calls and arithmetic are the existing ones in the existing order.
  - The review helper and fingerprint reach the feature through the sampler's mask, with no signature change (T6-3 escalation 2 does not arise).
  - The scatter jobs' halo tree mirror (`MirrorTreeAcceptance`) must call the same gated functions.
- **Proof:** `forest_structure_test`:
  - the class mix follows age across 64 forest tiles (mean height rank rises monotonically over age quintiles);
  - feature off is byte-identical;
  - the probe's per-pose numbers are reproduced within 2 %.

### A3. Species by habitat

The weights are computed after spacing, only for accepted candidates, from the canonical **coarse** level of the T5 habitat grid at the root. The tree tile's job evaluates coarse nodes lazily through one `RingHabitatFieldGrid` per tile. The 8 m fine level is never needed for trees. Inputs: M and T from the root's own `TerrainSample`; S from the root's ±2 m slope; W, R64 and X from a new grid method, `SampleCoarse`.

- **`SampleCoarse`:** `RingHabitatFieldGrid.SampleCoarse(double around, double across)` reads the bilinear coarse nodes whether or not the cell is fine-flagged. It is pure, so tiles and layers agree.
  - It does not change `Sample`.
  - The probe measured **2.1-3.2 ms** for a whole coarse window over a 240 m tile plus 16 m (194-209 heights and 110-121 climate samples).
  - Plains tiles with few trees pay little.

```
maple_h = ss(0.30,0.65,M) * (0.4 + 0.6 ss(0.30,0.70,W)) * (1 - 0.5 ss(0.15,0.50,R64)) * (1 - 0.6 ss(12,28,S)) * ss(0.30,0.45,T)
fir_h   = (1 - ss(0.32,0.55,T)) * (0.5 + 0.5 ss(0.40,0.70,M))                               // cool and high, toward the treeline
pine_h  = 0.8 (1 - ss(0.25,0.60,M)) + 0.6 ss(0.10,0.50,R64) ss(10,25,S) + 0.5 X             // dry, convex and rocky ground
h = maple_h + fir_h + pine_h  (if h < 1e-4: maple only)
w_s = 0.75 * species_h / h + 0.25 * regional.{Maple,Fir,Pine}Weight                          // P11 pockets stay as secondary variation
```

- **Selection:** the generation 1 hash (`salt + 3`) is compared against the cumulative `w_s`.
  - "Sandy" ground has no field before T7. Dryness and convexity stand in for it, and T7's dune provinces can add a term.
  - "Near water" sharpens once T5-11 fills `ChannelWetness`, since W is `WetnessTotal`.
- **Probe result:** at the three forest poses these weights give maple 1.00, the same as generation 1. The poses are moist, warm lowland with no fir or pine pockets (generation 1 records: 300 / 185 / 70 maples). Conifers appear where the climate and relief call for them.
- **Proof:** `forest_structure_test` (maple share rises with W, pine with dryness and R64, fir with cold; feature off identical).

### A4. Treeline

- **Density:** `tau_d(T) = 0.15 + 0.85 SmoothStep(0.24, 0.40, T)` multiplies the chance (A2).
  - The default biome profiles put Tundra at T 0-0.28 (blend width 0.12) and Forest from 0.28. The vegetated biomes therefore end at T of about 0.22, and the band 0.24-0.40 is the transition.
- **Size:** effective age is multiplied by `SmoothStep(0.24, 0.45, T)` (A2), so the classes fall toward the smallest.
- **Species:** maple is cut by `ss(0.30, 0.45, T)` and fir rises with `1 - ss(0.32, 0.55, T)`. The last trees are fir medium (4.2 m) and pine small (4-4.7 m).
- `ForestRegionSampler`'s own `climateCore` (temperature) still applies underneath, unchanged.
- **Monotonicity test** (`forest_structure_test`): over the 64 tiles plus 16 treeline tiles (found by a scan for T 0.22-0.45 with Forest or Grassland biome), stems per hectare and mean tree height, binned in steps of 0.03 in T, are non-decreasing in T. A bin may dip by at most one standard error.

### A8. Shrubs

- **Source rectangles.** For each pack's foliage surface of LOD0 (all variants of the species), the tool rasterizes the UV triangles into a 256 x 256 usage map of the cluster atlas and takes the connected components with an area of at least 0.5 % of the atlas. The bounding rectangles, shrunk to the alpha bounds at a cutoff of 0.25, are the "cluster rectangles". Each shrub look uses the 6-12 rectangles with the most usage.
  - It is deterministic: the components are sorted by area, then by the minimum corner.
  - The rectangles go into the derived manifest.
- **Mesh (generated, per look, four variants each, indices 43-54).**
  - **Broadleaf (maple clusters):** a dome of 14 cards.
    - 6 vertical cards on 3 crossed planes at 0, 60 and 120 degrees, each split into lower and upper rectangles.
    - 6 outward-tilted cards (35 degrees) around the dome.
    - 2 near-horizontal top cards.
    - Height 1, width 1.1. Hash jitter of ±15 % per card in size and offset. Vertex normals bent outward from the dome centre (the TR-7 crown-normal idea), so the shrub shades as a volume.
  - **Pine scrub (pine clusters):** 10 cards, lower and wider (height 1, width 1.4), with tilted cards dominant (45 degrees).
  - **Fir thicket (fir boughs):** 12 cards in 3 tiers of near-horizontal boughs (15-25 degrees down), conical (height 1, base width 1.2).
  - **LOD1:** 3 crossed vertical cards plus 1 top card, using the look's largest rectangles.
  - Wind attachments: sway weight = height fraction squared, foliage mode 1.
- **Shading.** They draw through the understory carrier (A7). The order's "imported_tree.gdshader foliage mode" is replaced by the carrier shader, which is built on the same includes and proven equal on saplings by `understory_render_match_gpu_test`. Wind, haze, shadows, wetness and forest lighting apply as on tree foliage, with no separate switch.

### A9. Tint

- **Per instance:** v_i = 2 fract(`INSTANCE_CUSTOM.z` x 4271.0) - 1 and h_i = 2 fract(z x 7919.0 + 0.37) - 1, where z is the rank, identical for the mesh and impostor rows of one root (`metadata.x`).
- **Per stand:** a shader field. Roots have no free channel (P12), and a CPU value would need a new root word.
  - **Coordinates.** A new global `ringworld_foliage_frame` (mat4) is built each frame with `HeightShadowFrameMath.BuildFrame` at an anchor: the camera's canonical position snapped to 4,096 m. With it, global `ringworld_foliage_tint` (vec4): x = strength, y = the anchor's around mod 65,536 m, z = the anchor's across, w = 1 when published.
  - **Field.** The shader takes the root's offset `frame * root_world` (float32, about 0.5 mm within 6 km). It adds the anchor (exact integers below 2^17), so the canonical position is known to about 8 mm at 70 km. Value noise on 512 m cells with an integer PCG hash of the wrapped cell indices gives v_s in [-1, 1]. The pattern repeats every 65.5 km and has one invisible discontinuity at the ring seam.
  - It is published by the decoration layer only in worlds with bit 6.
- **Formula** (foliage only: cutout surfaces on meshes; at or above the crown base on the impostor, the FL-2c gate):
  - `ALBEDO *= (1 + s (0.08 v_i + 0.06 v_s)) * vec3(1 + s 0.03 (h_i + v_s), 1, 1 - s 0.06 (h_i + v_s))`, where s is the strength;
  - that is at most ±14 % in value and a small yellow-green shift, within the natural spread between stands.
  - The same function (`ring_foliage_tint`, in `ring_forest_lighting.gdshaderinc`) is used in `imported_tree.gdshader`, `imported_tree_impostor.gdshader` and `ring_understory.gdshader`, so the LOD2 to LOD3 hand-off cannot pop.
- **Gate:** `if (ring_world_has(6) && ringworld_foliage_tint.w > 0.5)`. Generation 1 shaders compute the same pixels when it is off; T6-8 proves it against a pre-capture (the FL-4 lesson: keep the existing statements verbatim and unconditional).
- **Menu key:** "Foliage colour variation" (A10).

  - **No anchoring halo.** `Anchor = NearTrees` with a 50 m nearest-tree search would mirror about 770 halo tree candidates per tile (an estimated 10-20 ms more). Rows take species from the A3 field instead, so saplings follow the local canopy species with no halo.
  - The 8 m exact lattice was measured as an alternative to exact heights: bilinear error p99 of 0.10 m on forest tiles, but 0.5 m on hills, and the window costs 6.8-8 ms (probe). It is cheaper only above about 1,000 records per tile, so it is rejected; exact heights stay, as for rocks.

  | output | camera draws | shadow draws (2 cascades) |
  |---|---|---|
  | saplings L0, L2 | 4 | |
  | shrubs L0, L1 | 4 | |
  | dead wood near, far | 4 | |
  | dead-wood caster (snags and logs, the LODF and near forms, within the sun map) | | 2 |

  - Very High and up add the sapling caster (+2, Q5).

## T6 design B: ground-cover cards, grass response to the fields, far canopy

T6-2, claude-lead, 2026-11-19. Same basis as design A.

### Summary B

- **New card kinds** 3-6: flowers, ferns and tall grass, with 6 reserved for reeds; 2 stays retired.
  - They substitute a share of main cards on the same lattice and cap: zero extra instances, **zero extra draws** (the same card mesh and material).
  - The decode must change in **two** places: the card shader and the GPU culling compute (`ring_gpu_vegetation_indirect.glsl:160` also reads `kind > 0`). Kinds 0 and 1 stay identical.
- **A second procedural atlas:** a `Texture2DArray`, one 1024 x 512 layer per kind with 4 variants, the A channel a petal or seed-head mask. 8.4 MB of VRAM for three layers. Its own generator version and cache key.
- **Grass reads the coarse habitat fields:**
  - thinner under closed canopy (to 0.25x);
  - up to 1.35x taller in wet hollows, up to 0.65x on dry crests;
  - bed and bar terms reserved.
  - Measured cost: **0.14-0.25 ms per 32 m tile** on forest, lowland and plains tiles; 0.5-0.66 ms on hills and steep tiles (probe).
- **Far canopy:**
  - the canopy cover splits the vegetated weight into forest and grass (forest weight = cover), in C# and GLSL behind bit 8;
  - the 64-bit hash is ported exactly with `umulExtended`;
  - past 4.5-5.5 km a canopy albedo with roughness 1 replaces the ground in new proxy and far shader variants;
  - grass density is unchanged by the fold; only the card colour follows the folded ground.

### B1. Kinds and decode

- **`RingGrassCardKind`:** `Card = 0, Low = 1, Flower = 3, Fern = 4, TallGrass = 5, Reed = 6` (reed reserved for T6-12; 2 is never reused). `CUSTOM.x = kind * 4 + variant + rank * 0.999` stays. For slot 27 (6 x 4 + 3) the rank's resolution is 2^-18, enough for the rank tiers.
- **Decode** (`ring_grass_cards.gdshader:90-95` and the compute's `card_keeps` call, `ring_gpu_vegetation_indirect.glsl:160`): `bool low = abs(kind - 1.0) < 0.5;` and in the shader `bool cover = kind > 2.5;`.
  - For kinds 0 and 1 the boolean is the same as `kind > 0.5`, and nothing else changes on their path.
  - Cover kinds take the main-card keep, grow-in and compensation. They are main-lattice cards, so their rank tiers and distance thinning behave like grass, and a meadow keeps its mean colour with distance.
  - `ground_cover_cards_test`'s identity case and the pre-capture prove the identity (T6-9 step 2).
- **Heights** (before compensation; the transform's Y scale), and widths:

  | kind | height | width |
  |---|---|---|
  | flower | 0.25-0.55 m | 0.35-0.6 m |
  | fern | 0.35-0.9 m | 0.7-1.4 m (fronds spread) |
  | tall grass | 0.7-1.2 m | 0.45 + 0.9 x height |
  | reed (T6-12) | 1.2-2.2 m | 0.4-0.7 m |

- **Bounds stay as they are.** `BoundsHorizontalExtent` and `BoundsHeightScale` multiply each instance's own transform (`BuildCardBounds`), and the compute's card guard scales with the instance axes (p[76], p[77]). A 1.2 m card gets proportionally larger bounds automatically, so no constant becomes per kind. (T6-12's escalation "reeds need bounds beyond B1" is therefore only about the height range.)

### B2. Atlas

- **Generator:** `RingGroundCoverAtlasGenerator` (`scripts/Voxel/LOD/`), `GeneratorVersion = 1`, with `RingGroundCoverAtlasParameters` and its own `CacheKey()`. It is cached like `RingGrassCardAssets`, in memory, built on the thread pool. `RingGrassCardAtlasGenerator`, `GeneratorVersion = 2` and `VariantD` are untouched.
- **Layers:** a `Texture2DArray`, layer = kind - 3, each 1024 x 512 (4 variants of 256 x 512), RGBA8, full mip chain.
  - The reed layer is generated by T6-12 under its own layer version. Each layer's hash is pinned, so layers 0-2 stay byte-identical when it lands.
  - VRAM: 2.8 MB per layer.
- **Channels:**
  - R: coverage (alpha-scissor mask);
  - G: height gradient (as grass);
  - B: per-element random;
  - **A: the petal mask** for flowers, the seed-head mask for tall grass, 0 for ferns.
- **Drawing (integer RNG, fixed loop order, like the grass generator):**
  - **Flowers:** grass-like stems and lanceolate leaves; heads per variant:
    - v0 white umbel or daisy (flat radial petals);
    - v1 yellow cup cluster (buttercup);
    - v2 violet-blue bells along the stem (campanula);
    - v3 pink-magenta raceme (fireweed, loosestrife).
  - **Ferns:** 5-9 arching fronds from the root. Each frond is a midrib curve with alternating tapering pinnae; v3 is a triangular, bracken-like tripinnate frond.
  - **Tall grass:** blades from the grass generator plus heads (A mask): v0 sedge (no heads), v1 feathery panicle, v2 spike, v3 drooping panicle.
- **Mips:** R uses the existing coverage-preserving mip routine (shared, not copied). A is mipped as the area mean, so far petals average into the colour.
- **Colour.**
  - Leaves and stems use the existing terrain-derived blade colour with a per-kind constant multiplier (fern (0.85, 1.0, 0.80): darker and bluer green; tall grass tips shifted toward straw by the A mask, 0.75, 0.68, 0.45).
  - Petals: `mix(blade, petal[variant] * (1 + 0.08 (2 y - 1)), A)`, with y = `INSTANCE_CUSTOM.y`. The base petal albedos (linear) are white (0.70, 0.70, 0.64), yellow (0.72, 0.55, 0.06), violet (0.24, 0.20, 0.50) and pink (0.55, 0.18, 0.40), inside the measured range of real petals. The petal colour then gets the same haze, night and wetness factors as the blade (`grass_card_finish_albedo`).
- **Palette rule (community and hash).** The community sets the variant weights (B3), and the variant fixes the colour family, as flower species do.
- **Shader:** a second sampler, `grass_cover_atlas : sampler2DArray`. Cover kinds read `vec3((variant + UV.x) * 0.25, UV.y, kind - 3.0)`; kinds 0 and 1 keep the existing read verbatim.

### B3. Selection

- **Where.** On the **main lattice only**, in `TryPlace` after the existing accept (chance, slope, water, exclusions) and before `CardShape`, only when the world has bit 7:
  - `u = Hash01(aroundCell, acrossCell, seed, salt + 23)`; the candidate becomes a cover kind if `u < share(community) * GroundCoverShare`.
  - The kind comes from `Hash01(..., salt + 29)` over the community's kind split, and the flower variant from `Hash01(..., salt + 31)` over its variant weights (salts 23, 29 and 31 are unused today).
  - Ferns, flowers and tall grass take their own heights (B1) after B4's multipliers.
- **Budget.** The instance count is unchanged by construction: one accepted candidate gives one card of some kind. The low layer and generation 1 candidates are untouched.
- **Community:** the A5 memberships at the candidate, from B4's 4 m patch lattice. The shares below are blended by membership weight, and "grassland" is 1 minus the sum of the memberships.

| community | cover share | fern / flower / tall | flower variants (white / yellow / violet / pink) |
|---|---|---|---|
| ClosedBroadleaf | 0.45 | .75 / .10 / .15 | .7 / .2 / .1 / 0 |
| ConiferStand | 0.25 | .60 / .10 / .30 | .5 / .3 / .2 / 0 |
| OpenWoodland | 0.30 | .20 / .30 / .50 | .35 / .30 / .20 / .15 |
| ForestEdge | 0.35 | .20 / .35 / .45 | .35 / .30 / .20 / .15 |
| ClearingCommunity | 0.35 | .10 / .50 / .40 | .2 / .2 / .1 / .5 |
| DryMeadow | 0.12 | 0 / .80 / .20 | .2 / .4 / .4 / 0 |
| WetMeadow | 0.40 | 0 / .30 / .70 (reeds in T6-12) | .1 / .5 / .1 / .3 |
| RockyFell | 0.05 | 0 / 1 / 0 | .3 / .4 / .3 / 0 |
| grassland (no community) | 0.10 | 0 / .60 / .40 | .3 / .4 / .2 / .1 |

- In plain grassland 6 % of cards are flower tufts; in clearings, 17 %. These are summer values; seasons are out of scope (plan 6).
- **Proof:** `ground_cover_cards_test`:
  - instance counts equal with and without ground cover;
  - kinds only in their communities (the membership above 0.05 at each cover card);
  - feature off: byte-identical card buffers at the eight fingerprint grass tiles;
  - the atlas is deterministic;
  - kind 2 is rejected (T6-9 step 2).

### B4. Grass follows the fields

- **How the 32 m tile gets fields** (T5 A4 "recompute"): the builder makes one `RingHabitatFieldGrid` on its worker sampler and evaluates `SampleCoarse` (design A3) at the 9 x 9 nodes of a canonical 4 m patch lattice over the tile.
  - The 4 m lattice divides 32 m, so it is pure per position. The derived fields (wetness, memberships) are computed at those nodes and read bilinearly per candidate.
  - No fine nodes are needed: grass responds at 32-64 m scales.
  - Measured (probe, the 4 coarse corners plus their 16 taps plus 64 point reads): **0.14-0.25 ms** on forest, plains, coast and lowland tiles; **0.50-0.66 ms** on hills and steep tiles.
  - Against today's 2.2-4.6 ms grass tiles that is +4-11 % on forest and lowland, and up to +15-25 % on hills and steep ground. There grass is sparse, and the T6-9 escalation (+20 % median) is watched per pose.
- **Formulas** (bit 7 only; generation 1 arguments are unchanged):
  - **density:** `chance *= 1 - 0.75 * ss(0.35, 0.80, Canopy)`. Under closed canopy 25 % of the grass chance remains, of which 45 % is then ferns (B3): a litter floor with fern patches.
  - **height:**
    - `h *= (1 + 0.35 ss(0.45, 0.85, Wetness)) * (1 - 0.35 ss(0.15, 0.60, Relief64) * (1 - ss(0.35, 0.65, Moisture)))`;
    - the low layer takes the height factor only.
  - **beds and bars:** the reserved factor `1 - ss(..., ChannelWetness / HeightAboveChannel)` is 1 until T5-11, and T6-12 (A11) sets it.
  - **Chance source.** In worlds with bit 8 the chance uses the *natural* biome weights (B5): the canopy response is owned here, not doubled by the fold.
- **Monotone checks** (`ground_cover_cards_test`): over the eight fingerprint tiles plus six dressing tiles, the instance count per canopy bin is non-increasing, and the mean card height per wetness bin is non-decreasing.

### B5. Far canopy

- **The fold** (C# `scripts/Vegetation/RingForestCanopyFold.cs`; GLSL `rc_forest_canopy_fold` in `ring_regional_climate.glslinc`):
  - `cover = SmoothStep(0.05, 0.55, ForestRegionSampler.Sample(around, across, sample, 0f).Density) * tau_d(T)`. The density is the generation 1 sampler at slope 0; `tau_d` is the A4 treeline.
  - `veg = 1 - (desert + tundra + alpine)`; `forest' = veg * cover`; grass is the remainder.
  - So the forest weight *is* the canopy cover: forest floor under trees, grass in clearings and meadows, near and far alike. There are no seams, because Terrain3D, the proxies and the far terrain all fold.
  - **Call sites:**
    - the coordinate overloads `GetBiomeTextureWeights(long, int)` and `GetLandBiomeTextureWeights` fold when the sampler's mask has bit 8;
    - the grass colour grid (`RingGrassTileBuilder` `GridWeights`) and the far colour (`RingTerrainVisuals.ResolveMacroTextureColor` where the call has coordinates) fold explicitly;
    - the **habitat grid keeps natural weights**, so rock exposure, scree and the rock records never move with bit 8.
  - **Slope.** The fold ignores slope, because the weight overloads have none. The shading term below fades by the shading normal's slope over 14-32 degrees (the sampler's ramp), and T4's rock takes steep ground where ground is present.
- **The GLSL port.**
  - **Hash.** The kernels evaluate `ForestRegionSampler`'s three value noises (12,288, 2,048 and 768 m) and the suitability. `Hash01` is exact in 32-bit arithmetic:
    - `rc_u64_mul(uvec2 a, uvec2 b)`: `umulExtended(a.x, b.x, hi, lo)` and `hi += a.x * b.y + a.y * b.x`. That is the low 64 bits, identical to C#'s unchecked `ulong` product.
    - Shifts and XORs on `uvec2`.
    - `(h >> 40)` as `uint`, times the float literal written from the exact bits of `1f / 16777215f`.
    - Around is canonical: `(anchor + dA) mod circumference`, an int below 2^25.
  - **Float arithmetic.** Fade, lerp, smoothstep and the division `(around - cellStart) / cellWidth` are all `precise`, so no FMA contraction (RyuJIT does not contract).
  - **Biome id.** `IsVegetatedBiome` and the 0.58 factor take the biome from the winning profile's channel: Forest is channel 1, Grassland has no channel.
    - `PackRecipe` asserts that this mapping is one to one.
    - If a custom biome resource breaks it, the fold is off on the GPU path and the parity test reports it as an escalation, never a silent mismatch.
  - **Feature mask.** The recipe buffer gains `#define RC_P_WORLD_FEATURES 317` (`RC_P_COUNT` 318), written by `PackRecipe`. The parse is at `ProxyGpuHeightKernel.cs:65`. The word is 0 in generation 1, so its weights are unchanged (the parity tests' feature-off rows).
  - **Kernels.** K1 (points with flag bit 0) and K2 (sector biome threads) call the fold after `rc_biome_weights`.
- **The shading term past the impostor range.**
  - Variants follow T4 D7's rule. The shipped proxy and far shaders are frozen generation 1 code, so new variant files carry `#define RING_FAR_CANOPY`:
    - `ring_terrain_proxy_canopy.gdshader` and `ring_terrain_proxy_ground_canopy.gdshader`;
    - `ring_terrain_global_canopy.gdshader` and `ring_terrain_global_ground_canopy.gdshader`.
  - `RingGroundSurfaceVariants` chooses by (ground, canopy). If the user freezes vegetation together with ground (Q7), only the `_ground` files gain the block, behind `ring_world_has(8)`, and no new files are needed.
  - **Term:** `w = COLOR.g (forest') * ss(far_start - 1000, far_start, dist) * (1 - ss(14, 32, slope_deg))`, where `far_start` is the published tree far distance (`MaximumLod5DistanceMeters`, 5,500 m). Then `ALBEDO = mix(ALBEDO, ringworld_far_canopy.rgb, w)`, `ROUGHNESS = mix(ROUGHNESS, 1.0, w)` and `SPECULAR *= 1 - w`.
  - **Canopy albedo.** `ringworld_far_canopy.rgb` comes from the impostor albedo layers at load: the alpha-weighted mean over the 30 layers, weighted by the landscape class shares (A2), times a canopy self-shading factor of 0.55. Closed canopies reflect about half the visible light their leaves do (shadowed gaps and lower leaves). T6-10 then calibrates only this factor, so the mean luminance of the tinted proxy at 5.6 km equals the impostor-rendered forest at 5.4 km within 10 % in the fly-out. That is a match condition, not a boost.
  - Conifer variation at range is left out: the weight carries no species, and a species channel would widen the generation 1 vertex format.
- **Grass:** the fold changes grass *colour* inside the card reach (cards match the folded ground under them), which is intended. It never changes grass *density* (B4).
- **Boundary test** (`far_canopy_tint_gpu_test`): on transects across a forest edge at 2, 5 and 20 km from the camera, the 0.5 contour of the rendered tint or forest weight must lie within one proxy vertex spacing at that distance of the C# expected-stem contour (half the interior stem density). Feature off leaves the far pixels identical.

### Questions for the user

1. **The understory's GPU cost.** Saplings, shrubs and dead wood together are estimated at 0.15-0.35 ms on your 4070 (High) and about 0.3-0.6 ms on the A1000 (Medium), over the plan's 0.3 ms A1000 gate. **Recommendation:** a budget of at most 0.35 ms on the 4070 at the three forest poses (1080p, High, your files) and at most 16 draws. The A1000 gate stays yours to run, and an overrun comes back to you before any density is cut. It is partly paid back by the thinner grass under the canopy, and later by occlusion culling.
2. **Build time.** In forest-structure worlds every forested tree tile builds about 2-3 ms slower (habitat species), and tiles within about 500 m run an understory job of 15-40 ms. A per-tile 1.3x gate cannot hold (as with rocks). **Recommendation:** read it as T5 Q1: per-pose fill totals (an estimated +15-20 % on forest fills), zero travel coverage waits, and stills with the table before any change. A cheaper alternative is species from climate and slope only (no relief term), which costs about 0 ms but loses "maple in hollows, pine on crests".
3. **Stand-age contrast** changes where and how big trees are, so it is a world-creation setting (a recipe field, default 1), not a grave-key slider. **Recommendation:** yes.
4. **Understory textures.** Species texture arrays let saplings, shrubs and dead wood draw in 6 batches instead of about 50. **Recommendation:** foliage at 1024² and bark at 512², about 10.5 MB of VRAM. 512² foliage would halve it but blur saplings within about 5 m.
5. **Shadows.** **Recommendation:** snags and fallen trees cast sun shadows at every preset (2 draws); saplings only at Very High and up (2 more draws), as plan 8 keeps per-instance vegetation shadows off below Very High; shrubs never.
6. **Shrub reach of 150 m** (plan 8 said 250 m): this keeps the 3,000 cap and the cost when you stand at an edge belt. Inside forest the understory hides anything farther. **Recommendation:** accept. Edge belts seen across a meadow end at 150 m, with a 20 m dither.
7. **Freeze order.** Vegetation reads the habitat grid, which uses T4's rock, scree and relief functions. If vegetation freezes before ground and rocks (today's order), those functions freeze with it, and any later ground tuning must keep a versioned copy (the fingerprint would catch a slip). **Recommendation:** do the G3 forest review in the review world, and freeze vegetation in the same step as ground and rocks (after the T8 rock textures).
