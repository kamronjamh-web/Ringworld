### Relief that the surface has to work with (T0 spec 8.1)

- R1. Slopes above 39 degrees (today's dirt threshold) are rare outside mountains and breaks: 2D 4 m slope p95 is 7.7 degrees on plains, 21.9 rolling, 29.2 hill country, 38.8 breaks, 42.3 mountain; mountain is 33.1 % of the lattice. Rock will therefore appear mainly in mountains and breaks; scree (28-38 degrees) is what hill country will show.
- R2. The 2-30 m decade is 0.04-0.24 m RMS on lowland, so **curvature at 8 m is centimetre noise until T7**. The 30-300 m decade is 0.7-3.9 m RMS (plains to hill country). Colour variation must be driven by curvature at 64 m or coarser to show anything before T7.

### Cost, re-checked against T0 8.6

- C1. On the 4070 (non-authoritative) near terrain costs 2.3-4.2 ms at every pose, and 55-60 % of root GPU on open ground. It is the largest fixed cost wherever trees are absent, so a T4 regression costs everywhere. The noise floor is 0.00-0.03 ms, so deltas of 0.05 ms are resolvable on the 4070.
- C2. The plan gate (0.4 ms on the A1000 at a hill pose, 0 new draws) is still plausible: rock can reuse `biome_alpine_albedo`, which the Terrain3D shader already samples for every fragment (S2), and the proxy samples instead of dirt. The added samples are the detail normal (near only) and the colour-variation taps. **Re-scoped gate:** measure at every T0 pose-time, not only the hill pose, because near terrain is everywhere; the gate is 0.4 ms A1000 at every pose, with draw and primitive counts unchanged.
- C3. **How T4 measures cost.** Raster passes have no GPU markers. T4-2 adds a same-boot A-B-A case that flips the `ground_surface` bit (feature on, off, on) in a review world; the delta is the cost. The A1000 numbers are authoritative (P0 / 35 W). The A1000 T0 baseline is still missing (handoff open item 2); until it exists, report 4070 deltas as indicative and flag any 4070 delta above 0.1 ms at any pose to the primary. That threshold assumes the A1000 is at least four times slower on this pass, which is **unverified**.
